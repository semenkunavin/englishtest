Reported Speech: Statements — इनडायरेक्ट स्पीच कैसे बनाएँ (B1)

Reported speech (इनडायरेक्ट स्पीच) का मतलब है किसी की बात को अपने शब्दों में बताना, quote किए बिना। यह job interview, office में बातचीत और daily spoken English में बहुत काम आता है — जैसे HR को बताना कि पिछले manager ने क्या कहा था। Statement बताते समय tense, pronoun और time words अक्सर बदल जाते हैं।

  1. Reporting verb अक्सर said या told होता है। told के बाद person ज़रूरी है: told me; said के बाद person नहीं: said to me या सिर्फ़ said।
  2. Present tense को Past में बदलें (back-shift): am/is → was, are → were, do → did, will → would, can → could।
  3. Past simple अक्सर past perfect बनता है: worked → had worked। पर ज़रूरत न हो तो past simple रह भी सकता है, ख़ासकर बोलचाल में।
  4. Pronouns और possessive बदलें ताकि मतलब सही रहे: I → he/she, my → his/her, we → they।
  5. Time और place words बदलते हैं: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there, this → that।
  6. Direct statement में that जोड़ सकते हैं, पर ज़रूरी नहीं: She said (that) she was busy। Question words वाले statements में कोई inversion नहीं होता।

Examples

  • Priya said, "I am preparing for the interview."Priya ने कहा, "मैं interview की तैयारी कर रही हूँ।" (Direct)
  • Priya said that she was preparing for the interview.Priya ने कहा कि वह interview की तैयारी कर रही थी। (Reported)
  • The manager told Ravi, "You will get the offer tomorrow."Manager ने Ravi से कहा, "तुम्हें कल offer मिलेगा।" (Direct)
  • The manager told Ravi that he would get the offer the next day.Manager ने Ravi से कहा कि उसे अगले दिन offer मिलेगा। (Reported)
  • Anjali said, "I finished the report in Pune yesterday."Anjali ने कहा, "मैंने Pune में कल report पूरी की।" (Direct)
  • Anjali said that she had finished the report in Pune the day before.Anjali ने कहा कि उसने Pune में एक दिन पहले report पूरी कर ली थी। (Reported)

आम गलतियाँ

The manager said me that I was selected.
The manager told me that I was selected.
said के बाद सीधे person नहीं आता; person के साथ told me या said to me लगाएँ।
Rahul said that he is busy today.
Rahul said that he was busy that day.
Reporting verb past (said) है, तो is → was और today → that day होगा।
Sneha said that she will call me tomorrow.
Sneha said that she would call me the next day.
will → would और tomorrow → the next day — दोनों बदलना ज़रूरी है।

  5. Arjun said, "I am here now." → Arjun said that he was ___ then.
