Question Tags (It's cold, isn't it?) — आसान हिंदी में सीखें

Question tag एक छोटा सवाल होता है जो sentence के अंत में लगता है, जैसे It's cold, isn't it? इसका use तब होता है जब हम confirmation चाहते हैं या बातचीत को friendly बनाना चाहते हैं। Interview और daily spoken English में यह natural English की पहचान है।

  1. Main sentence positive हो, तो tag negative होता है: You are Rajesh, aren't you?
  2. Main sentence negative हो, तो tag positive होता है: You don't work here, do you?
  3. Tag में helping verb (is, are, do, have, will) और pronoun (he, she, it, they, you) आता है — noun नहीं: Priya is from Pune, isn't she?
  4. Simple present या past में helping verb न हो तो do / does / did use करें: You know Anil, don't you?
  5. I am के साथ tag है aren't I? — I am late, aren't I?
  6. Tag में falling tone = confirmation, rising tone = real question। Polite बातचीत में tone ज़रूर ध्यान दें।

Examples

  • It's very hot in Chennai today, isn't it?आज Chennai में बहुत गर्मी है, है ना?
  • You are from Jaipur, aren't you?आप Jaipur से हैं, है ना?
  • Ravi doesn't speak French, does he?Ravi French नहीं बोलता, है ना?
  • Priya worked in Mumbai last year, didn't she?Priya ने पिछले साल Mumbai में काम किया था, है ना?
  • You can join from Monday, can't you?आप Monday से join कर सकते हैं, है ना?
  • Let's discuss the salary, shall we?चलिए salary पर बात करें, ठीक है?

आम गलतियाँ

You are Amit, isn't it?
You are Amit, aren't you?
Tag का pronoun subject से match होना चाहिए — you के साथ aren't you, न कि isn't it।
She likes tea, doesn't she likes?
She likes tea, doesn't she?
Tag में main verb repeat नहीं होता — सिर्फ helping verb और pronoun आते हैं।
He is not coming, isn't he?
He is not coming, is he?
Negative sentence के बाद positive tag आता है।
