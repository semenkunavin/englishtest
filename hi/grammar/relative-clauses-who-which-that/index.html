Relative Clauses: who / which / that (Hindi mein seekhein)

Relative clause ek chhota sentence-part hota hai jo kisi person ya cheez ke baare mein extra information deta hai. Isme hum who, which aur that use karte hain. Job interview aur daily spoken English mein yeh bahut kaam aata hai, jaise apne experience ya skills ke baare mein batate waqt.

  1. Logon ke liye who use karein: the man who called.
  2. Cheezon aur animals ke liye which use karein: the job which I got.
  3. that logon aur cheezon dono ke saath chal sakta hai (spoken English mein common): the girl that sings.
  4. Relative clause noun ke turant baad aata hai: the company which hired me.
  5. Clause ke andar pronoun dobara na lagayein — the man who he called galat hai.
  6. Interview mein short clauses achhe lagte hain: I am a person who learns fast.

Examples

  • I want a job which uses my English skills.Mujhe aisi job chahiye jo meri English skills use kare.
  • She is the interviewer who asked me about my family.Woh interviewer hi hai jisne mujhse mere family ke baare mein poocha.
  • This is the company that called me for an interview.Yeh wahi company hai jisne mujhe interview ke liye call kiya.
  • Ramesh is the friend who helped me prepare for the interview.Ramesh woh dost hai jisne interview ki taiyari mein meri madad ki.
  • I like candidates who speak clearly.Mujhe aise candidates pasand hain jo saaf bolte hain.
  • The course which I did in Patna was very useful.Jo course maine Patna mein kiya tha, woh bahut useful tha.

आम गलतियाँ
