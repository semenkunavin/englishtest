Past Perfect (had done) — आसान हिंदी में सीखें

Past perfect का मतलब है — past में कोई काम पहले हो चुका था, उसके बाद दूसरा past काम हुआ। Formula है had + past participle (V3). Interview में "I had already completed my training before I joined" जैसे वाक्य इसी से बनते हैं।

  1. Structure: subject + had + V3 (past participle). जैसे She had finished the report.
  2. दो past actions में जो पहले हुआ, उसके लिए past perfect और जो बाद में हुआ उसके लिए simple past इस्तेमाल करें.
  3. Sequence clear करने के लिए before, after, already, by the time, never का इस्तेमाल करें.
  4. Negative: had not (hadn't) + V3 — जैसे He hadn't applied before the deadline.
  5. Question: Had + subject + V3 — जैसे Had you finished the interview round?
  6. बहुत पुरानी बात या थर्ड कंडीशनल (If I had known…) में भी past perfect आता है.

Examples

  • I had completed my internship in Jaipur before I applied for this job.मैंने इस job के लिए apply करने से पहले जयपुर में अपनी internship पूरी कर ली थी।
  • Priya had already left the office when the manager called.जब manager ने call किया, तब तक प्रिया office से जा चुकी थी।
  • Had you spoken to the HR before the interview?क्या interview से पहले आपने HR से बात की थी?
  • Rahul hadn't checked his email, so he missed the interview link.राहुल ने अपना email check नहीं किया था, इसलिए उसने interview link miss कर दिया।
  • By the time we reached Mumbai, the meeting had started.जब तक हम मुंबई पहुंचे, meeting शुरू हो चुकी थी।
  • She told the panel that she had worked in a startup for two years.उसने panel को बताया कि उसने एक startup में दो साल काम किया था।

आम गलतियाँ

I had completed my training before I had joined the company.
I had completed my training before I joined the company.
जो action बाद में हुआ (joined) उसके लिए simple past चाहिए, दोनों में had न लगाएँ.
She had went to Delhi for the final round.
She had gone to Delhi for the final round.
had के बाद V3 आता है — went नहीं, gone.
Did you had submitted your resume?
Had you submitted your resume?
Question में सिर्फ Had + subject + V3; did और had साथ नहीं आते.
