Much, Many, A Lot Of: आसान Hindi में English Grammar (A1)

यह topic बताता है कि English में मात्रा (quantity) कैसे बताते हैं। Much और many countable/uncountable nouns में फ़र्क़ करते हैं, और a lot of दोनों के साथ चलता है। Job interview और daily spoken English में quantity बताना बहुत common है।

  1. Many के साथ countable nouns (plural) आते हैं: many students, many calls, many people.
  2. Much के साथ uncountable nouns आते हैं: much time, much money, much work.
  3. A lot of दोनों के साथ चलता है — countable और uncountable दोनों: a lot of friends, a lot of experience.
  4. Positive sentences में a lot of ज़्यादा natural है; much अकेला positive sentence में कम आता है: I have a lot of work. (न कि I have much work.)
  5. Much और many mostly negative और questions में आते हैं: I don't have much time. / Do you have many questions?
  6. बिना noun के सीधे जवाब देना हो तो a lot लिखें: How much experience do you have? — A lot.
  7. How much = uncountable (price, time, money), How many = countable (people, years, projects)।

Examples

  • Ravi has a lot of experience in sales.रवि के पास sales में बहुत experience है।
  • There aren't many jobs in our town, so I am moving to Pune.हमारे town में ज़्यादा jobs नहीं हैं, इसलिए मैं Pune जा रहा हूँ।
  • How much time do you need for this project?इस project के लिए आपको कितना time चाहिए?
  • How many people work in your Delhi office?आपके Delhi office में कितने लोग काम करते हैं?
  • I don't have much money now, but I have a lot of skills.मेरे पास अभी ज़्यादा पैसा नहीं है, लेकिन बहुत skills हैं।
  • Priya asked a lot of questions in the interview.प्रिया ने interview में बहुत सारे questions पूछे।

आम गलतियाँ

I have much friends in Mumbai.
I have many friends in Mumbai.
Friends countable (plural) है, इसलिए many आएगा, much नहीं।
She has many experience.
She has a lot of experience.
Experience यहाँ uncountable है, इसलिए a lot of (या much negative/question में) सही है।
I have much work today.
I have a lot of work today.
Positive sentence में a lot of natural है; much अकेला positive में कम आता है।

खुद जांचें

  2. I don't have ___ time before the interview.

  3. Anil knows ___ people in Bangalore.

  4. How ___ money do you need for the course?

  5. There aren't ___ jobs in this small city.
