Past Simple of "to be" (was / were) — आसान Hindi में

Past simple of to be का मतलब है भूते काल में 'होना' — was और were। जब हम past की बात करते हैं — अपने बारे में, अपनी job, अपनी city या किसी person के बारे में — तब इनका use होता है। Interview में "I was a sales executive in Jaipur" जैसे sentences बहुत common हैं।

  1. I / he / she / it के साथ was लगाओ: I was, He was, Priya was, It was.
  2. you / we / they के साथ were लगाओ: You were, We were, They were, Rahul and Amit were.
  3. Negative में was not / were not (short form: wasn't / weren't): I wasn't late, They weren't ready.
  4. Question में was / were पहले आता है: Were you in Delhi? Was he your manager?
  5. Past of to be में helping verb नहीं लगती — सीधे was / were use करो: I was busy (✗ I did was busy).
  6. Short answer: Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.

Examples

  • I was a sales executive in Jaipur for two years.मैं दो साल तक Jaipur में sales executive था।
  • She was the team leader in her last company.वह अपनी last company में team leader थी।
  • We were very busy last Monday.पिछले Monday हम बहुत busy थे।
  • Were you at the interview in Mumbai?क्या तुम Mumbai में interview में थे?
  • The manager was not happy with the report.Manager report से happy नहीं था।
  • My friends were in Pune last week.मेरे friends पिछले हफ़्ते Pune में थे।

आम गलतियाँ

I were a teacher in Agra.
I was a teacher in Agra.
I के साथ हमेशा was आता है, were नहीं।
They was at the office.
They were at the office.
They plural है, इसलिए were लगेगा।
You did was late yesterday.
You were late yesterday.
to be के past में did नहीं लगती; सीधे were use करो।
