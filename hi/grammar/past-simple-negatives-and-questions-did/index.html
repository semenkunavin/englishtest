Past Simple के Negatives और Questions: did का Use

Past simple में जब हम past की बात करते हैं, तो negative और question sentences में did का use होता है। Interview में जब आपको पूछा जाए Did you work before? या आप कहें I didn't work yesterday, तो यही structure काम आता है। यह बहुत आसान है: did लगाओ, और main verb को अपने basic form में रखो।

  1. Negative: subject + did not (didn't) + verb की first form. जैसे She didn't call.
  2. Question: Did + subject + verb की first form? जैसे Did you apply for the job?
  3. सब subjects (I, you, he, she, we, they) के साथ did ही लगता है — did का form नहीं बदलता।
  4. did/didn't के बाद verb की second form (called, went) नहीं आती — सिर्फ first form (call, go) आती है।
  5. Short answers: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. — यहाँ did अकेला आता है, कोई verb नहीं।
  6. Wh-question में पहले question word, फिर did: Where did you study? / Why didn't you come?

Examples

  • I didn't attend the interview yesterday.मैंने कल interview attend नहीं किया।
  • Did you send your resume to the company?क्या आपने company को अपना resume भेजा?
  • Ramesh didn't speak English in the meeting.Ramesh ने meeting में English नहीं बोली।
  • Did Priya get the job in Pune?क्या Priya को Pune में job मिली?
  • We didn't have time for practice.हमारे पास practice के लिए time नहीं था।
  • Where did you learn spoken English?आपने spoken English कहाँ सीखी?

आम गलतियाँ

I didn't went to the office.
I didn't go to the office.
didn't के बाद verb की first form आती है, second form नहीं।
Did you called the manager?
Did you call the manager?
Did के बाद भी verb की first form रहती है।
He didn't came for the interview.
He didn't come for the interview.
Past का meaning पहले से didn't में है, इसलिए come ही सही है।

खुद जांचें

  1. ___ you speak to the HR manager?

  2. They didn't ___ the training.

  3. Where ___ you work before?
