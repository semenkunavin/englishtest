Second Conditional: If I had more time, I would travel (B1)

Second conditional का use हम unreal या imagination वाली situation के लिए करते हैं — जो अभी true नहीं है। Structure है If + past simple, फिर would + verb. Job interview में जवाब देते time और spoken English में यह बहुत common है, जैसे "If I had more time, I would learn new skills."

  1. If वाले part में past simple लगाओ: If I had, If she worked, If we lived.
  2. दूसरे part में would + base verb (verb की first form) लगाओ: would travel, would learn.
  3. यह unreal situation है — meaning present या future का है, past का नहीं। इसलिए past form देखकर confuse मत होओ।
  4. I / he / she / it के साथ भी were formal English में सही है: If I were you.
  5. would के बाद to नहीं आता: would travel ✔, would to travel ✘.
  6. Polite request और advice के लिए भी useful: If I were you, I would apply for this job.

Examples

  • If I had more time, I would travel across India.अगर मेरे पास ज़्यादा समय होता, तो मैं पूरे India में घूमता।
  • If Priya lived in Pune, she would walk to office.अगर Priya Pune में रहती, तो वह office तक पैदल जाती।
  • If I knew English fluently, I would speak up in meetings.अगर मुझे English fluently आती, तो मैं meetings में बोलता।
  • If Rahul had a car, he would drive to Delhi for the interview.अगर Rahul के पास car होती, तो वह interview के लिए Delhi car से जाता।
  • If I were you, I would take this job offer.अगर मैं तुम्हारी जगह होता, तो मैं यह job offer ले लेता।
  • We would save more money if we cooked at home.अगर हम घर पर खाना बनाते, तो हम ज़्यादा पैसे बचाते।

आम गलतियाँ

If I would have more time, I would travel.
If I had more time, I would travel.
If वाले part में would नहीं आता; वहाँ past simple (had) लगता है।
If she works harder, she would get the promotion.
If she worked harder, she would get the promotion.
Second conditional में If के बाद past simple चाहिए — works नहीं, worked।
I would to apply for this job.
I would apply for this job.
would के बाद to नहीं आता, सीधे base verb (apply) आता है।
