Present Perfect vs Past Simple: आसान Hindi में समझें

यह topic बताता है कि Present Perfect (have/has + V3) और Past Simple (V2) में क्या फर्क है। Interview में अपने experience, projects और achievements बताते समय यह सही use करना बहुत ज़रूरी है। Simple rule: past की exact time बतानी हो तो Past Simple, और अभी तक का result या experience बताना हो तो Present Perfect।

  1. Past की exact time दी हो (yesterday, last year, in 2020, two days ago) तो हमेशा Past Simple लगाओ: I joined Infosys in 2020.
  2. Time नहीं दी और बात अभी भी relevant है, तो Present Perfect लगाओ: I have joined a new company.
  3. Life experience बताने के लिए Present Perfect use होता है: She has worked in Mumbai and Pune.
  4. Finished time के साथ Present Perfect कभी नहीं आता — I have met him yesterday गलत है, I met him yesterday सही है।
  5. for + duration और since + starting point के साथ Present Perfect: I have worked here for three years. / I have worked here since 2021.
  6. Interview में अक्सर दोनों साथ आते हैं: पहले experience (Present Perfect), फिर detail (Past Simple) — I have managed a team of ten. I led a project in Bengaluru last year.

Examples

  • I have worked as a sales executive for two years.मैंने दो साल तक sales executive के रूप में काम किया है (और अभी भी कर रहा हूँ)।
  • I worked as a sales executive in Jaipur in 2019.मैंने 2019 में Jaipur में sales executive के रूप में काम किया (past की exact time)।
  • Priya has just finished her interview at TCS.Priya ने अभी-अभी TCS में अपना interview पूरा किया है।
  • Rahul passed his engineering exam last month.Rahul ने पिछले महीने अपना engineering exam पास किया।
  • Have you ever spoken to a client in English?क्या तुमने कभी किसी client से English में बात की है?
  • We met the HR manager yesterday at the Delhi office.हम कल Delhi office में HR manager से मिले थे।

आम गलतियाँ

I have met the manager yesterday.
I met the manager yesterday.
yesterday finished time है, इसलिए Past Simple चाहिए, Present Perfect नहीं।
I am working here since 2021.
I have worked here since 2021.
Starting point के साथ Present Perfect + since आता है, Present Continuous नहीं।
Did you ever visit Chennai?
Have you ever visited Chennai?
Life experience पूछने के लिए have you ever + V3 use होता है, did नहीं।

  2. She ___ her interview at Wipro last Friday.

  3. ___ you ever worked with a foreign client?

  4. We ___ the project two days ago.

  5. He ___ just sent his resume to the Mumbai office.
