Passport control in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

Ye phrases aapko airport par passport control (immigration) counter par kaam aayenge. Job interview aur spoken English ki practice ke liye bhi yehi simple, common sentences bahut useful hain.

काम के वाक्यांश

  • May I see your passport, please?Kya main aapka passport dekh sakta hoon, please?
  • Here you go.Ye lijiye.
  • What is the purpose of your visit?Aapke visit ka purpose kya hai?
  • I am here for tourism.Main tourism ke liye aaya hoon.
  • I am here to visit my family.Main apne family se milne aaya hoon.
  • I am here on a student visa.Main student visa par aaya hoon.
  • How long are you going to stay?Aap kitne din rukne wale hain?
  • I will stay for two weeks.Main do hafte rukunga.
  • I am staying for ten days.Main das din ruk raha hoon.
  • Where will you be staying?Aap kahan thehrenge?
  • I will stay at a hotel in Delhi.Main Delhi ke ek hotel mein rukunga.
  • I am staying with my relatives in Jaipur.Main Jaipur mein apne relatives ke saath ruk raha hoon.
  • Here is my return ticket.Ye mera return ticket hai.
  • Do you have a return ticket?Kya aapke paas return ticket hai?
  • Is this your first visit to the UK?Kya ye UK ki aapki pehli visit hai?
  • No, I visited last year.Nahi, main pichhle saal aaya tha.
  • Are you travelling alone?Kya aap akela travel kar rahe hain?
  • Yes, I am travelling alone.Haan, main akela travel kar raha hoon.
  • Could you say that again, please?Kya aap ise dobara keh sakte hain, please?
  • Thank you. Have a nice stay.Thank you. Aapka stay achha ho.

नमूना बातचीत

  • सामने वाला: Good morning. Passport, please.Good morning. Passport, please.
  • आप: Good morning. Here you go.Good morning. Ye lijiye.
  • सामने वाला: What is the purpose of your visit?Aapke visit ka purpose kya hai?
  • आप: I am here for tourism. I want to see London and Edinburgh.Main tourism ke liye aaya hoon. Main London aur Edinburgh dekhna chahta hoon.
  • सामने वाला: How long are you going to stay?Aap kitne din rukne wale hain?
  • आप: I will stay for two weeks.Main do hafte rukunga.
  • सामने वाला: Where will you be staying?Aap kahan thehrenge?
  • आप: I will stay at a hotel in London. I have the booking here.Main London ke ek hotel mein rukunga. Booking yahan hai.
  • सामने वाला: Do you have a return ticket?Kya aapke paas return ticket hai?
  • आप: Yes, here it is. My flight back is on 20 May.Haan, ye hai. Mera return flight 20 May ko hai.
  • सामने वाला: Thank you. Have a nice stay.Thank you. Aapka stay achha ho.
  • आप: Thank you very much.Thank you very much.

आम गलतियाँ

I am here for tour.
I am here for tourism. / I am here on a tourist visa.
"Tour" noun hai, purpose ke liye "tourism" ya "tourist visa" bolna natural hai.
I will stay two weeks.
I will stay for two weeks.
Duration ke saath "for" lagana zyada clear aur natural lagta hai.
I am staying in my uncle house.
I am staying at my uncle's house.
Possessive mein apostrophe-s chahiye, aur house ke saath "at" aata hai.

टिप

Officer ke sawal ka seedha jawab dein — short aur clear sentence. Agar samajh na aaye to politely bolein: "Could you say that again, please?" Ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi; documents (booking, return ticket) haath mein rakhna aapko confident banata hai — interview mein bhi yehi habit kaam aati hai.
