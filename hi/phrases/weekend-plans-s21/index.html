Weekend plans in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

Yeh phrases tab kaam aate hain jab aap dost ke saath weekend ka plan discuss karte ho — kya karna hai, kahan milna hai, aur time kya fix karna hai. Job interview aur daily spoken English dono mein aise simple phrases bahut useful hote hain.

काम के वाक्यांश

  • What are you doing this weekend?Is weekend aap kya kar rahe ho?
  • Do you have any plans for Saturday?Saturday ke liye koi plan hai kya?
  • I'm free on Sunday afternoon.Main Sunday afternoon free hoon.
  • Let's do something fun.Chalo kuch mazedaar karte hain.
  • How about going to a movie?Movie dekhne chalna kaisa rahega?
  • That sounds good to me.Mujhe yeh accha lagta hai.
  • I'd rather stay at home.Main ghar par hi rehna prefer karunga.
  • Are you free in the evening?Shaam ko free ho kya?
  • Let's meet at the metro station.Chalo metro station par milte hain.
  • What time works for you?Aapke liye kaunsa time theek hai?
  • Let's meet around 5 o'clock.Chalo 5 baje ke aas-paas milte hain.
  • Shall we go by auto or by bus?Auto se jaayein ya bus se?
  • I'll book the tickets online.Main online tickets book kar dunga.
  • Can you check the timings?Aap timings check kar sakte ho?
  • I'm not sure yet.Main abhi sure nahi hoon.
  • Let me confirm by Friday.Main Friday tak confirm kar dunga.
  • Sorry, I can't make it on Saturday.Sorry, main Saturday ko nahi aa paunga.
  • Can we plan for Sunday instead?Sunday ke liye plan kar sakte hain kya?
  • Great, see you on Sunday!Badhiya, Sunday ko milte hain!
  • I'll message you the details.Main aapko details message kar dunga.

नमूना बातचीत

  • सामने वाला: Hi Rahul, what are you doing this weekend?Hi Rahul, is weekend aap kya kar rahe ho?
  • आप: Nothing much. I'm free on Sunday afternoon. Why?Kuch khaas nahi. Main Sunday afternoon free hoon. Kyun?
  • सामने वाला: Let's do something fun. How about going to a movie?Chalo kuch mazedaar karte hain. Movie dekhne chalna kaisa rahega?
  • आप: That sounds good to me. Which movie?Mujhe yeh accha lagta hai. Kaunsi movie?
  • सामने वाला: The new Hindi movie at PVR in Saket. It's getting good reviews.Saket ke PVR mein nayi Hindi movie. Uski reviews acchi aa rahi hain.
  • आप: Nice. What time works for you?Badhiya. Aapke liye kaunsa time theek hai?
  • सामने वाला: Let's meet around 5 o'clock at the metro station.Chalo 5 baje ke aas-paas metro station par milte hain.
  • आप: Okay. I'll book the tickets online. Can you check the timings?Theek hai. Main online tickets book kar dunga. Aap timings check kar sakte ho?
  • सामने वाला: Sure. I'll message you the details by Friday.Zaroor. Main Friday tak aapko details message kar dunga.
  • आप: Great, see you on Sunday!Badhiya, Sunday ko milte hain!

आम गलतियाँ

I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
English mein 'agree' khud verb hai, iske saath 'am' nahi lagta.
How about go to a movie?
How about going to a movie?
'How about' ke baad verb mein -ing form aata hai.
I will meet you on Sunday at 5 pm clock.
I will meet you on Sunday at 5 o'clock.
Time ke liye 'o'clock' bolte hain, 'pm clock' nahi.

टिप

Plan fix karte waqt teen cheezein clear rakho: kya karna hai, kahan milna hai, aur kitne baje. Yeh teen details English mein bolne se aap confident lagte ho — interview aur office mein bhi yahi style kaam aata hai.
