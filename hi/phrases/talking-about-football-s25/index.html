Football in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

Yeh phrases tab kaam aate hain jab aap doston ke saath football par chat karte hain ya interview mein apne favourite sport ke baare mein bolte hain. Simple English se aap kisi bhi conversation mein confidently hissa le sakte hain.

काम के वाक्यांश

  • Which team do you support?Aap kis team ko support karte hain?
  • I'm a big fan of Mumbai City FC.Main Mumbai City FC ka bada fan hoon.
  • Did you watch the match last night?Kya aapne kal raat ka match dekha?
  • What was the score?Score kya tha?
  • It was two-one.Do-one tha.
  • Who scored the goal?Goal kisne kiya?
  • They played really well in the second half.Unhone dusre half mein bahut accha khela.
  • Their defence was strong.Unka defence strong tha.
  • The referee made a bad call.Referee ne galat decision liya.
  • He got a red card.Usse red card mila.
  • My favourite player is Sunil Chhetri.Mera favourite player Sunil Chhetri hai.
  • He's a great striker.Woh ek great striker hai.
  • Who do you think will win the final?Aapko kya lagta hai final kaun jeetega?
  • I think Bengaluru FC will win.Mujhe lagta hai Bengaluru FC jeetega.
  • They have a strong team this season.Is season unki team strong hai.
  • I usually watch matches with my friends.Main aksar matches apne doston ke saath dekhta hoon.
  • Let's watch the next match together.Chaliye agla match saath mein dekhte hain.
  • Which position does he play?Woh kis position par khelta hai?
  • He plays as a goalkeeper.Woh goalkeeper ke roop mein khelta hai.
  • That was an amazing save!Woh ek amazing save tha!

नमूना बातचीत

  • सामने वाला: Hi Priya! Did you watch the match last night?Hi Priya! Kya aapne kal raat ka match dekha?
  • आप: Yes, I did. It was very exciting.Haan, maine dekha. Bahut exciting tha.
  • सामने वाला: What was the score?Score kya tha?
  • आप: It was two-one. Mumbai City won.Do-one tha. Mumbai City jeeti.
  • सामने वाला: Who scored the goals?Goals kisne kiye?
  • आप: Sunil Chhetri scored one, and their winger scored the second.Sunil Chhetri ne ek kiya, aur unke winger ne dusra kiya.
  • सामने वाला: Which team do you support?Aap kis team ko support karte hain?
  • आप: I support Mumbai City FC. And you?Main Mumbai City FC ko support karta hoon. Aur aap?
  • सामने वाला: I'm a big fan of Bengaluru FC. I think they have a strong team this season.Main Bengaluru FC ka bada fan hoon. Mujhe lagta hai is season unki team strong hai.
  • आप: True. Let's watch the next match together at my place.Sahi. Chaliye agla match mere ghar par saath mein dekhte hain.
  • सामने वाला: Great idea! I'll bring snacks.Great idea! Main snacks laaunga.

आम गलतियाँ

I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
Agree ek verb hai, iske saath am nahi lagta.
I support to Mumbai City FC.
I support Mumbai City FC.
Support ke baad seedha team ka naam aata hai, to nahi lagta.
Last night I watched a match of football.
Last night I watched a football match.
English mein 'football match' bolte hain, 'match of football' nahi.
