Daily routine, sleep aur health in English: phrases aur sample dialogue

Yeh phrases tab kaam aate hain jab aap apne daily routine, sleep schedule aur exercise habits ke baare mein English mein baat karte hain. Job interview mein aur office ya gym ki casual baat-cheet mein yeh bahut useful hain.

काम के वाक्यांश

  • I usually wake up around six in the morning.Main aam taur par subah chhe baje ke aas-paas uthta hoon.
  • I get ready and have breakfast by seven.Main taiyar hokar saat baje tak breakfast kar leta hoon.
  • I leave for work at half past eight.Main sava aath baje kaam ke liye nikalta hoon.
  • I take the metro to the office.Main office jaane ke liye metro leta hoon.
  • I try to sleep at least seven hours.Main kam se kam saat ghante sone ki koshish karta hoon.
  • I go to bed before eleven.Main gyarah baje se pehle so jaata hoon.
  • I work out three times a week.Main hafte mein teen baar exercise karta hoon.
  • I do yoga in the morning.Main subah yoga karta hoon.
  • I go for a walk after dinner.Main dinner ke baad walk par jaata hoon.
  • I feel fresh after a good sleep.Achhi neend ke baad main fresh feel karta hoon.
  • How many hours do you sleep?Aap kitne ghante sote hain?
  • Do you exercise regularly?Kya aap regular exercise karte hain?
  • What time do you usually get up?Aap aam taur par kitne baje uthte hain?
  • I have a busy routine on weekdays.Weekdays par mera routine busy rehta hai.
  • I take a short break in the afternoon.Main dopahar mein thoda break leta hoon.
  • I skip sweets and fried food.Main sweets aur fried food avoid karta hoon.
  • I drink a lot of water during the day.Main din mein kaafi paani peeta hoon.
  • I want to be fit and healthy.Main fit aur healthy rehna chahta hoon.
  • My routine helps me stay focused at work.Mera routine mujhe kaam par focus rakhne mein madad karta hai.
  • Can you suggest a simple routine for me?Kya aap mujhe ek simple routine suggest kar sakte hain?

नमूना बातचीत

  • सामने वाला: Hi Rohit, good to see you. Let's talk about your daily routine.Hi Rohit, aapko dekhkar achha laga. Chaliye aapke daily routine ki baat karte hain.
  • आप: Sure. I usually wake up at six and go for a short walk in the park.Zaroor. Main aam taur par chhe baje uthta hoon aur park mein thodi walk par jaata hoon.
  • सामने वाला: That's a good start. How many hours do you sleep every night?Yeh achhi shuruaat hai. Aap har raat kitne ghante sote hain?
  • आप: Around six hours. Sometimes I sleep late because of work calls.Lagbhag chhe ghante. Kabhi-kabhi kaam ke calls ki wajah se late sota hoon.
  • सामने वाला: Try to get seven hours of sleep. Do you exercise during the week?Saat ghante sone ki koshish kijiye. Kya aap hafte mein exercise karte hain?
  • आप: Not regularly. I want to start gym in the morning, but I feel tired.Regular nahi. Main subah gym shuru karna chahta hoon, lekin thakaan feel hoti hai.
  • सामने वाला: Start with twenty minutes of yoga three days a week.Hafte mein teen din bees minute yoga se shuru kijiye.
  • आप: That sounds easy. What about food habits?Yeh aasaan lagta hai. Khane ki aadaton ke baare mein kya?
  • सामने वाला: Have a light dinner before nine and skip sweets at night.Nau baje se pehle halka dinner lijiye aur raat mein sweets avoid kijiye.
  • आप: Okay, I will follow this routine and sleep before eleven.Theek hai, main yeh routine follow karunga aur gyarah baje se pehle so jaunga.
  • सामने वाला: Perfect. You will feel fresh and focused at work.Perfect. Aap kaam par fresh aur focused feel karenge.

आम गलतियाँ

I am wake up at six daily.
I wake up at six every day.
Daily routine ke liye simple present use karein, aur 'wake up' ke saath 'am' nahi lagta.
I sleep from seven hours.
I sleep for seven hours.
Duration ke liye 'from' nahi, 'for' lagta hai — jaise 'for seven hours'.
I am going to gym three time in a week.
I go to the gym three times a week.
Habit ke liye simple present chahiye, 'time' ki jagah 'times' aur gym se pehle 'the' aata hai.

टिप

Apna ek chhota daily routine English mein likhkar roz boliye — jaise 'I wake up at six, I sleep for seven hours, I walk in the evening.' Isse interview mein confidently bolna aasaan ho jaayega.
