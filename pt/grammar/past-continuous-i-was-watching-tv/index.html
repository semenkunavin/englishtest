Past continuous: I was watching TV

O past continuous é o tempo verbal que usamos para falar de uma ação que estava em andamento no passado, como I was watching TV. Você usa quando quer descrever o que estava acontecendo em um momento específico ou quando outra ação aconteceu. É muito útil para contar histórias, falar de viagens e de situações no trabalho.

  1. Forma: was/were + verbo com -ing. Use was com I, he, she, it e were com you, we, they: She was studying, They were traveling.
  2. Negativa: coloque not depois de was/were: I was not (wasn't) sleeping, We weren't working.
  3. Pergunta: inverta o was/were e o sujeito: Were you watching TV? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
  4. Use o past continuous para uma ação em andamento e o past simple para a ação que a interrompeu: I was cooking when the phone rang.
  5. Use while com o past continuous para dizer "enquanto": While I was studying, Ana was working.
  6. Verbos de estado, como know, want e like, normalmente não vão para o -ing: diga I wanted to travel, não I was wanting to travel.

Exemplos

  • Yesterday at 8 p.m., Júlia was watching TV in her apartment in Curitiba.Ontem às 20h, a Júlia estava assistindo TV no apartamento dela em Curitiba.
  • I was preparing for an interview when my boss called me.Eu estava me preparando para uma entrevista quando meu chefe me ligou.
  • While we were traveling in Salvador, it was raining every afternoon.Enquanto a gente estava viajando em Salvador, estava chovendo toda tarde.
  • They were not sleeping at midnight; they were studying English.Eles não estavam dormindo à meia-noite; estavam estudando inglês.
  • Were you working at the office in São Paulo last week?Você estava trabalhando no escritório em São Paulo na semana passada?
  • Rafael was talking to a client when the internet stopped working.O Rafael estava falando com um cliente quando a internet parou de funcionar.

Erros típicos

I was watch TV.
I was watching TV.
Depois de was/were o verbo precisa estar em -ing.
She were studying English.
She was studying English.
Com he, she e it usamos was, não were.
While I was cooking, the phone was ringing.
While I was cooking, the phone rang.
A ação curta que interrompe vai no past simple: rang.

Teste você mesmo

  1. I ___ watching TV when you called.

  2. They ___ traveling in Recife last week.

  3. ___ you working at the office yesterday?

  4. Camila ___ not sleeping at midnight; she was studying.

  5. While I was preparing my CV, my father ___ dinner.
