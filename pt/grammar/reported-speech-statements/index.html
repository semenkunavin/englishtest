Reported speech: statements — discurso indireto em inglês

O reported speech (discurso indireto) serve para contar o que outra pessoa disse, sem usar aspas: She said (that) she was busy. Você vai usar isso o tempo todo no trabalho — ao repassar um recado de um cliente em uma reunião — e em viagens, ao explicar o que o recepcionista do hotel falou para a sua família.

  1. Use say sem objeto (He said (that) he was tired) e tell com objeto (He told me (that) he was tired). A palavra that é opcional.
  2. Recue o tempo verbal um passo: present → past ("I work in São Paulo" → She said she worked in São Paulo), past → past perfect ("I saw Ana" → He said he had seen Ana), will → would ("I will call you" → She said she would call me).
  3. Mude os pronomes e os possessivos para manter o sentido: "I lost my ticket" → Ela disse que perdeu o ingresso dela → She said she had lost her ticket.
  4. Ajuste as expressões de tempo e lugar: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there.
  5. Can vira could, may vira might e must normalmente vira had to: "I must finish the report" → He said he had to finish the report.
  6. Se a informação continua verdadeira, o tempo verbal pode ficar igual: She said she lives in Curitiba.

Exemplos

  • Marina said she had sent the budget to the client the day before.A Marina disse que tinha enviado o orçamento para o cliente no dia anterior.
  • My manager told me he would give me feedback after the meeting.Meu gerente me disse que me daria um feedback depois da reunião.
  • Paulo said he was working on a project in Recife that week.O Paulo disse que estava trabalhando em um projeto em Recife naquela semana.
  • The receptionist told us our room was on the fifth floor.O recepcionista nos disse que o nosso quarto ficava no quinto andar.
  • Ana said she couldn't find her passport at the airport.A Ana disse que não conseguia encontrar o passaporte dela no aeroporto.
  • Thiago said he had to book his flight to Salvador that day.O Thiago disse que tinha que reservar o voo dele para Salvador naquele dia.

Erros típicos

She said me that she was late.
She told me that she was late.
say não leva objeto indireto; com me use tell.
He said he will call me tomorrow.
He said he would call me the next day.
Depois de um verbo no passado, will vira would e tomorrow vira the next day.
They told that the tour started at nine.
They said that the tour started at nine.
tell precisa de um objeto: told us; sem objeto, use said.

Teste você mesmo

  1. Carla said she ___ the email two days before.

  2. My boss ___ me that the interview was on Monday.

  3. "I can help you." → Bruno said he ___ help me.

  4. Renata said she ___ finish the report that day, so she had to stay late.
