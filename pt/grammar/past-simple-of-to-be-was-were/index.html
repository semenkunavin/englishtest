Past simple de to be: was e were (nível A1)

O past simple de to be é was / were e significa "era", "estava" ou "foi" no passado. Você usa para falar de situações, lugares e sentimentos em um momento passado: uma viagem, uma reunião, um emprego anterior. É o passado mais comum para descrever como as coisas eram antes.

  1. Use was com I, he, she, it e substantivos no singular: I was in Recife.
  2. Use were com you, we, they e substantivos no plural: They were at the airport.
  3. Negativa: was not / were not ou contraído wasn't / weren't: She wasn't at work yesterday.
  4. Pergunta: coloque was ou were antes do sujeito: Were you in São Paulo last week?
  5. Respostas curtas: Yes, I was. / No, they weren't. Não repita o resto da frase.
  6. Para idade e sentimentos no passado também: I was 25, We were tired after the flight.

Exemplos

  • I was in Salvador on business last month.Eu estava em Salvador a negócios no mês passado.
  • Mariana was very tired after the flight to Lisbon.A Mariana estava muito cansada depois do voo para Lisboa.
  • We were at a job interview in Curitiba yesterday.Nós estávamos em uma entrevista de emprego em Curitiba ontem.
  • They weren't at the office on Friday.Eles não estavam no escritório na sexta-feira.
  • Was your first job in Rio de Janeiro?O seu primeiro emprego foi no Rio de Janeiro?
  • The hotels were expensive during the holiday in Florianópolis.Os hotéis estavam caros durante o feriado em Florianópolis.

Erros típicos

I were at the airport.
I was at the airport.
Com I usa-se was, não were.
She didn't was at the meeting.
She wasn't at the meeting.
O verbo to be não usa didn't; a negativa é wasn't / weren't.
Were you at the hotel? Yes, I were.
Were you at the hotel? Yes, I was.
Na resposta curta, repita a forma correta do verbo: Yes, I was.

Teste você mesmo

  1. Paulo ___ in São Paulo last week for a training course.

  2. We ___ at the airport at 6 a.m. yesterday.

  3. ___ you at the conference in Brasília?

  4. The flights ___ expensive last month.

  5. Ana ___ not at work on Monday. She was in Belo Horizonte.
