Present Continuous (I am working) — A1 English Lesson

The present continuous describes an action happening now, at this moment. You make it with am/is/are + a verb ending in -ing. Use it for things in progress today, right now, or around this time.

  1. Form: am/is/are + verb-ing. Use am with I, is with he/she/it, are with you/we/they.
  2. Examples: I am working, she is reading, they are playing. Short forms: I'm, he's, they're.
  3. Negative: put not after am/is/are: I am not working, she isn't reading, they aren't playing.
  4. Questions: put am/is/are before the subject: Are you working? Is he reading? What are they doing?
  5. Spelling: most verbs add -ing (work → working). Verbs ending in -e drop the e (write → writing). Short verbs double the consonant (run → running, sit → sitting).
  6. Do not use the present continuous with state verbs like like, know, want. Say I like coffee, not I am liking coffee.

Examples

  • I am working in the office today.I am working in the office today.
  • She is reading a book right now.She is reading a book right now.
  • They are playing football in the park.They are playing football in the park.
  • We are not watching TV at the moment.We are not watching TV at the moment.
  • Are you listening to me?Are you listening to me?
  • He is writing an email to his boss.He is writing an email to his boss.

Typical mistakes

I working now.
I am working now.
The present continuous needs the verb am/is/are before the -ing form.
She are reading a book.
She is reading a book.
Use is with he, she and it, not are.
He is runing in the park.
He is running in the park.
Short verbs ending in one vowel + one consonant double the consonant: run → running.
