Be going to for Plans — A1 English Lesson

We use be going to to talk about plans we made before now. For example, you can say what you will do tomorrow, next week or next year. The form is am / is / are + going to + verb.

  1. Use am with I: I am going to travel.
  2. Use is with he, she, it: She is going to study.
  3. Use are with you, we, they: They are going to move.
  4. After going to, use the base verb (no to, no -ing): going to buy, not going to buying.
  5. For questions, put be before the subject: Are you going to come?
  6. For negatives, add not after be: He is not going to work.

Examples

  • I am going to visit my grandmother on Sunday.I have already decided to visit my grandmother on Sunday.
  • She is going to start a new job next month.She has a plan to start a new job next month.
  • We are going to have dinner at seven.We plan to have dinner at seven.
  • They are not going to buy a car this year.They have decided not to buy a car this year.
  • Are you going to study tonight?I am asking about your plan for tonight.
  • He is going to play football after school.He has a plan to play football after school.

Typical mistakes

I going to travel to Spain.
I am going to travel to Spain.
The verb be (am) is necessary before going to.
She is going to studies English.
She is going to study English.
After going to, use the base verb, not the -s form.
We are going to the beach going.
We are going to go to the beach.
The pattern is going to + base verb; do not leave out the main verb.
