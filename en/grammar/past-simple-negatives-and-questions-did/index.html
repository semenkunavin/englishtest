Past Simple: Negatives and Questions with Did (A1)

We use the past simple to talk about finished actions in the past. For negatives and questions we use the helper word did. After did and didn't, the main verb stays in the base form.

  1. Negative: subject + didn't + base verb. Example: She didn't call.
  2. Question: Did + subject + base verb? Example: Did you call him?
  3. After did and didn't, use the base verb, not the past form: didn't go, not didn't went.
  4. Didn't is the short form of did not. Both are correct; didn't is more common in speaking.
  5. Short answers: Yes, I did / No, I didn't. Do not repeat the main verb.
  6. Use did for all persons: I did, she did, they did. The form never changes.

Examples

  • I didn't watch TV last night.I didn't watch TV last night.
  • Did you finish your homework?Did you finish your homework?
  • He didn't go to work yesterday.He didn't go to work yesterday.
  • Did she like the film?Did she like the film?
  • We didn't have lunch at home.We didn't have lunch at home.
  • Did they arrive on time? Yes, they did.Did they arrive on time? Yes, they did.

Typical mistakes

She didn't went to the party.
She didn't go to the party.
After didn't, use the base verb, not the past form.
Did you saw the film?
Did you see the film?
After did, use the base verb, not the past form.
Did he cooked dinner?
Did he cook dinner?
The past is already in did, so the main verb stays in the base form.
