Articles a / an / the: Easy Guide for A1 Learners

Articles are small words that come before nouns: a, an and the. We use a or an for one thing when it is not specific, and the when we talk about a specific thing. We often do not use an article with plural nouns and with general ideas.

  1. Use a before a singular noun that starts with a consonant sound: a book, a car, a university.
  2. Use an before a singular noun that starts with a vowel sound: an apple, an egg, an hour.
  3. Use the when the listener knows which thing you mean: the sun, the door, the book on my desk.
  4. Use the for the second mention of a thing: I have a dog. The dog is small.
  5. Do not use a or an with plural nouns or uncountable nouns: books, water (not a books, a water).
  6. For jobs and descriptions, use a or an: She is a teacher. He is an engineer.

Examples

  • I have a cat and a dog.I have one cat and one dog, not specific.
  • She eats an orange every morning.Orange starts with a vowel sound, so we use 'an'.
  • Please close the window.There is one window here, so both people know it.
  • This is a nice hotel. The hotel is near the beach.First mention: 'a'; second mention: 'the'.
  • He is a doctor.We use 'a' to say someone's job.
  • I like music and tea.No article for general or uncountable ideas.

Typical mistakes

I have a apple.
I have an apple.
'Apple' starts with a vowel sound, so we use 'an'.
She is teacher.
She is a teacher.
A singular job noun needs 'a' or 'an'.
I see a dog. A dog is big.
I see a dog. The dog is big.
The second time we mention the dog, we use 'the'.

Check yourself

  1. I need ___ umbrella. It is raining.

  2. There is ___ book on the table. It is mine.

  3. Please open ___ door.

  4. He has ___ egg for breakfast.

  5. She is ___ nurse.
