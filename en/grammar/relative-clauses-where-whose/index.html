Relative clauses: where and whose (B1 English)

Relative clauses add information about a noun. We use where to talk about places and whose to talk about things that belong to someone. These words join two ideas into one clear sentence.

  1. Use where after a place noun (a city, a shop, a house). It means 'in which' or 'at which'.
  2. Use whose after a person or thing noun to show possession. It means 'of whom' or 'of which'.
  3. Whose is always followed by a noun: whose car, whose name, whose children.
  4. Do not use that or which instead of where when the meaning is 'in which': say the town where I live.
  5. In defining relative clauses, do not put a comma before where or whose.
  6. Use whose for people and things, but never add 's after it: the man whose dog, not 'whose dog's'.

Examples

  • This is the café where we first met.This is the café in which we first met.
  • I want to visit the town where my grandmother grew up.I want to visit the town in which my grandmother grew up.
  • That's the shop where you can buy fresh bread.That's the shop at which you can buy fresh bread.
  • She is the student whose essay won the prize.She is the student; her essay won the prize.
  • I live in a house whose windows face the sea.I live in a house; its windows face the sea.
  • We met a man whose job is training pilots.We met a man; his job is training pilots.

Typical mistakes

This is the park where I play football there.
This is the park where I play football.
With where, do not repeat the place with 'there'.
She is the girl who car was stolen.
She is the girl whose car was stolen.
Use whose to show possession, not who.
The city that I was born is very old.
The city where I was born is very old.
Use where, not that, to mean 'in which' for places.

Check yourself

  1. This is the restaurant ___ we had dinner last night.

  2. I have a neighbour ___ son plays in my football team.

  3. The village ___ my parents live is quiet and beautiful.

  4. She wrote a book ___ title I can't remember.

  5. Do you know a shop ___ they sell old maps?
