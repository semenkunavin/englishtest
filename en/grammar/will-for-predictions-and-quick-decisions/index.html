Will: Predictions and Quick Decisions (A2 English)

We use will to talk about the future. We use it for predictions (guesses about what will happen) and for quick decisions made at the moment of speaking. The form is the same for all persons: I will, you will, he will, and so on.

  1. Use will + base verb for all persons: I will go, she will go, they will go.
  2. For predictions, will shows what you think or guess about the future: It will rain tomorrow.
  3. Use will for a decision you make now, while you are speaking: I'm tired. I will go to bed.
  4. Negative: will not or won't + base verb: He won't come.
  5. Questions: Will + subject + base verb: Will you help me? The short answer is Yes, I will / No, I won't.
  6. Use I think, I'm sure, maybe with will for predictions: I think she will like the gift.

Examples

  • I think it will be sunny tomorrow.I think it will be sunny tomorrow.
  • Don't worry, I will help you with your bags.Don't worry, I will help you with your bags.
  • She won't be late — she always comes on time.She won't be late — she always comes on time.
  • The phone is ringing. I will answer it.The phone is ringing. I will answer it.
  • Will you come to my party on Saturday?Will you come to my party on Saturday?
  • I'm sure our team will win the game.I'm sure our team will win the game.

Typical mistakes

She will goes to the party.
She will go to the party.
After will we use the base verb, never the -s form.
I will call you yesterday.
I will call you tomorrow.
Will talks about the future, so a past time word like yesterday is wrong.
Do you will help me?
Will you help me?
Questions with will do not use do. Put will before the subject.

  4. ___ you help me with this box, please?
