Verbs with Prepositions: depend on, listen to (B1)

Some English verbs always come with a particular preposition, and that pair works as one unit. You cannot guess the preposition from your own language, so you need to learn each pair as a whole. This page shows the most useful ones at B1 level: depend on, listen to, wait for, look at and belong to.

  1. Learn the verb and the preposition together as one item: depend on, listen to, wait for, look at, belong to.
  2. The preposition is always followed by a noun or an -ing form, never by a base verb: depend on the weather, listen to music, good at listening to people.
  3. The preposition does not change with the tense or the person: I depend on it, she depended on it, they are depending on it.
  4. In questions and relative clauses the preposition usually stays at the end: What are you listening to? / the job (that) I applied for.
  5. Do not add a second preposition or drop the preposition: listen to me (not listen me), depend on it (not depend of it).
  6. Object pronouns go after the preposition: listen to him, wait for us, look at them — not between the verb and the preposition.

Examples

  • Our holiday plans depend on the weather.Our holiday plans depend on the weather.
  • Please listen to the instructions carefully.Please listen to the instructions carefully.
  • We waited for the bus for twenty minutes.We waited for the bus for twenty minutes.
  • Look at this photo — do you recognise the place?Look at this photo — do you recognise the place?
  • I don't know who this umbrella belongs to.I don't know who this umbrella belongs to.
  • She's very good at listening to other people's problems.She's very good at listening to other people's problems.

Typical mistakes

I always listen music in the car.
I always listen to music in the car.
listen needs the preposition to before its object.
It depends of the price.
It depends on the price.
The fixed pair is depend on, not depend of.
We are waiting the results.
We are waiting for the results.
wait is followed by for before the thing you expect.

Check yourself

  1. The final result depends ___ how hard you work.

  2. Can you listen ___ me for a moment?

  3. I'm waiting ___ my sister at the station.

  4. Look ___ that building — it's over 500 years old.

  5. What are you listening ___?
