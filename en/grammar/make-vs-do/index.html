Make vs Do: How to Choose the Right Verb

English has two very common verbs for actions: make and do. We use make when we create or produce something, and do for activities, tasks and jobs. The verb you need depends on the noun that follows it, so many combinations must be learned as fixed phrases.

  1. Use make when you create or produce something new: make a cake, make a plan, make a mistake.
  2. Use do for activities, tasks and jobs: do homework, do the housework, do business.
  3. Use make with money and results: make money, make a profit, make a decision.
  4. Use do with general words like something, anything, nothing: do something useful, do nothing.
  5. Some phrases are fixed and must be memorised: make a phone call but do the washing-up.
  6. Do not translate from your own language: learn make and do as pairs with their nouns.

Examples

  • She always makes a shopping list before going to the supermarket.She always makes a shopping list before going to the supermarket.
  • I have to do my homework before dinner.I have to do my homework before dinner.
  • They made a big decision about their future last week.They made a big decision about their future last week.
  • Can you do me a favour and close the window?Can you do me a favour and close the window?
  • He makes money by selling old bicycles online.He makes money by selling old bicycles online.
  • We usually do the washing-up together after lunch.We usually do the washing-up together after lunch.

Typical mistakes

I need to do a phone call to my mother.
I need to make a phone call to my mother.
Make is used with a phone call; it is a fixed phrase.
She made her homework very quickly.
She did her homework very quickly.
Homework is a task, so we use do.
We must do a decision today.
We must make a decision today.
Make goes with a decision because you produce it.

Check yourself

  1. Could you ___ the washing-up tonight?

  2. She ___ a mistake in her email.

  3. I want to ___ something useful this afternoon.

  4. They ___ a lot of money last year.

  5. Please ___ your homework before you play.
