Past Simple: Common Irregular Verbs (A1 English)

The past simple talks about finished actions in the past, for example yesterday, last week, or two years ago. Many common English verbs are irregular, so they do not take -ed: go becomes went, and see becomes saw. You need to learn these forms by heart.

  1. Regular verbs add -ed (work → worked), but irregular verbs have a special form: go → went, see → saw, have → had, do → did, eat → ate, come → came.
  2. The past form is the same for all persons: I went, you went, he went, we went, they went.
  3. In negatives and questions, use did (or didn't) with the base verb, not the past form: I didn't go, Did you see it?
  4. With the verb be, use was (I, he, she, it) and were (you, we, they): I was tired, They were late.
  5. Time words like yesterday, last night, two days ago show that the action is finished.

Examples

  • I went to the park yesterday.I went to the park yesterday.
  • She saw her friends at the cinema.She saw her friends at the cinema.
  • We had breakfast at seven o'clock.We had breakfast at seven o'clock.
  • He did his homework last night.He did his homework last night.
  • They ate pizza for dinner.They ate pizza for dinner.
  • I didn't go to work on Monday.I didn't go to work on Monday.

Typical mistakes

I didn't went to school.
I didn't go to school.
After didn't, use the base verb go, not the past form went.
She goed to the shop.
She went to the shop.
Go is irregular: the past form is went, not goed.
Did you saw the film?
Did you see the film?
In questions with did, use the base verb see, not saw.
