Wish + Past Simple: Regrets and Unreal Situations (B1)

We use wish + past simple to talk about things in the present that we are not happy about and want to be different. It shows a regret or an unreal situation, not a real past event. For example, I wish I had more time means I don't have much time now.

  1. After wish, use the past simple to talk about the present: I wish I knew the answer (= I don't know it now).
  2. The past form does not mean past time here. It shows that the situation is unreal or imagined.
  3. With be, use were for all persons: I wish I were taller. In informal English, was is also common.
  4. Use wish + past simple about yourself or other people: She wishes she lived closer to work.
  5. Do not use wish + present simple for regrets: say I wish I had a car, not I wish I have a car.
  6. For past regrets, use wish + past perfect instead: I wish I had studied harder.

Examples

  • I wish I spoke French better.I want my French to be better than it is now.
  • We wish our flat were bigger.Our flat is small, and we want it to be bigger.
  • He wishes he didn't have to work on Saturdays.He has to work on Saturdays, and he doesn't like it.
  • I wish I were on holiday right now.I am not on holiday now, but I want to be.
  • She wishes her brother lived nearer.Her brother lives far away, and she wants him closer.
  • Do you wish you had more free time?You don't have much free time, and I'm asking if you want more.

Typical mistakes

I wish I have a bigger garden.
I wish I had a bigger garden.
After wish for a present situation, use the past simple, not the present simple.
I wish I am not so tired.
I wish I were not so tired.
Use were (or informal was) with wish, not am.
I wish I studied harder last year.
I wish I had studied harder last year.
For a regret about the past, use wish + past perfect, not the past simple.

Check yourself

  1. I wish I ___ a car. I hate taking the bus.

  2. She wishes she ___ near her family.

  3. I wish I ___ so shy at parties.

  4. He wishes he ___ the answer to this question.

  5. We wish we ___ more time to travel.
