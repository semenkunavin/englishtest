Reported Speech: Statements in English (B1)

Reported speech is how we tell someone what another person said. We use it in conversations, messages, stories and news when we repeat someone's words without quoting them directly. In statements, the verb usually moves one tense back and pronouns and time words often change.

  1. Use said or told + person: She said (that) she was tired. / She told me (that) she was tired.
  2. Move the verb one tense back: present → past, past → past perfect, will → would, can → could.
  3. Change pronouns to match the situation: "I am busy," he said. → He said he was busy.
  4. Change time and place words: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, here → there.
  5. The word that is optional after said and told.
  6. If the situation is still true, you can keep the original tense: She said she lives in London.

Examples

  • "I am very busy," she said.She said she was very busy.
  • "We will visit you tomorrow," they told me.They told me they would visit me the next day.
  • "I have finished my homework," he said.He said he had finished his homework.
  • "I can't come to the party," Anna said.Anna said she couldn't come to the party.
  • "I saw Tom here yesterday," Mark said.Mark said he had seen Tom there the day before.
  • "I want to help you now," my brother said.My brother said he wanted to help me then.

Typical mistakes

She said me that she was tired.
She told me that she was tired.
Say is not followed by a person, but tell is: tell somebody.
He said he is ill yesterday.
He said he was ill yesterday.
The verb usually moves one tense back: is → was.
I told that I would call her.
I said that I would call her.
Tell needs a person after it: told her, or use said.

Check yourself

  1. "I like coffee," she said. → She said she ___ coffee.

  2. "I will help you," he told me. → He told me he ___ help me.

  3. "I can swim," Tom said. → Tom said he ___ swim.

  4. "We are at the park," they said. → They said they were at the park ___.

  5. "I have lost my phone," she said. → She said she ___ her phone.
