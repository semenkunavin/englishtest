Present Simple: Affirmative Sentences (A1 English)

The present simple describes facts, habits and routines. In affirmative sentences we use the base verb, and we add -s with he, she and it.

  1. Use the base form of the verb with I, you, we, they: I work, they live here.
  2. Add -s to the verb with he, she, it: she works, he lives here.
  3. Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o take -es: he watches, she goes.
  4. Verbs ending in a consonant + -y change to -ies: study → he studies.
  5. Use have with I, you, we, they and has with he, she, it.
  6. Word order is subject + verb + the rest: My sister speaks English well.

Examples

  • I drink coffee every morning.I drink coffee every morning.
  • She works in a bank.She works in a bank.
  • We live in a small town.We live in a small town.
  • He watches TV in the evening.He watches TV in the evening.
  • My brother studies at university.My brother studies at university.
  • They have two children.They have two children.

Typical mistakes

She work in a bank.
She works in a bank.
With she the verb takes -s.
He watch TV every night.
He watches TV every night.
Verbs ending in -ch add -es with he.
My father have a car.
My father has a car.
With he, she, it we use has, not have.
