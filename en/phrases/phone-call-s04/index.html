Arranging a Meeting by Phone in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you arrange a meeting by phone when the person you call is busy. You will learn how to suggest another time and confirm the new arrangement.

Useful phrases

  • Hello, this is [your name] from [your company].Hello, this is [your name] from [your company].
  • I'm calling to arrange a meeting with [client's name].I'm calling to arrange a meeting with [client's name].
  • Is this a good time to talk?Is this a good time to talk?
  • I'm sorry, but I'm busy right now.I'm sorry, but I'm busy right now.
  • Could we arrange another time?Could we arrange another time?
  • When would be convenient for you?When would be convenient for you?
  • How about [day] at [time]?How about [day] at [time]?
  • That works for me.That works for me.
  • I'm afraid I can't make it then.I'm afraid I can't make it then.
  • Let me check my schedule.Let me check my schedule.
  • Would [day] be better?Would [day] be better?
  • I have a meeting at that time.I have a meeting at that time.
  • Can we postpone our meeting?Can we postpone our meeting?
  • Let's reschedule for next week.Let's reschedule for next week.
  • I'll send you an email to confirm.I'll send you an email to confirm.
  • Please let me know if that's okay.Please let me know if that's okay.
  • Thank you for your time.Thank you for your time.
  • I look forward to meeting you.I look forward to meeting you.
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you repeat?Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you repeat?
  • Have a good day. Goodbye.Have a good day. Goodbye.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Good morning, [Company Name]. How can I help you?Good morning, [Company Name]. How can I help you?
  • You: Hello, this is [Your Name] from [Your Company]. I'd like to arrange a meeting with Mr. Smith.Hello, this is [Your Name] from [Your Company]. I'd like to arrange a meeting with Mr. Smith.
  • Partner: I'm sorry, but Mr. Smith is busy at the moment. Can I take a message?I'm sorry, but Mr. Smith is busy at the moment. Can I take a message?
  • You: Yes, please. Could we arrange another time to meet?Yes, please. Could we arrange another time to meet?
  • Partner: Of course. When would be convenient for you?Of course. When would be convenient for you?
  • You: How about next Tuesday at 10 a.m.?How about next Tuesday at 10 a.m.?
  • Partner: Let me check. I'm afraid Mr. Smith has a meeting then. Would Wednesday at 2 p.m. work?Let me check. I'm afraid Mr. Smith has a meeting then. Would Wednesday at 2 p.m. work?
  • You: Yes, that works for me. Thank you.Yes, that works for me. Thank you.
  • Partner: Great. I'll send you an email to confirm.Great. I'll send you an email to confirm.
  • You: Perfect. I look forward to meeting Mr. Smith. Goodbye.Perfect. I look forward to meeting Mr. Smith. Goodbye.

Typical mistakes

I want to make a meeting.
I'd like to arrange a meeting.
Use 'arrange a meeting' (not 'make a meeting') and 'I'd like' for polite requests.
When are you free?
When would be convenient for you?
The second phrase is more polite and formal, which is better for phone calls with clients.
I call for arrange a meeting.
I'm calling to arrange a meeting.
Use the present continuous 'I'm calling' and the infinitive 'to arrange' after 'calling'.

Tip

Speak slowly and clearly on the phone. If you don't understand something, it's okay to ask the other person to repeat it.
