Talking about football in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you chat about football with friends, classmates or colleagues. Use them to talk about your favourite team and to describe a match you watched.

Useful phrases

  • Who do you support?Which team do you like and follow?
  • I'm a big fan of Liverpool.I really like and follow Liverpool.
  • I support my local team.I follow the team from my town or city.
  • Did you watch the match last night?Did you see the game yesterday evening?
  • What was the score?What was the result, with the number of goals?
  • It ended two–one.The final score was 2–1.
  • They won three–nil.They won 3–0.
  • It was a great game.The match was very good.
  • It was a boring match.The game was not interesting.
  • Who scored the goal?Which player got the goal?
  • He scored in the last minute.He got a goal at the end of the match.
  • That was a penalty.That goal came from a penalty kick.
  • The goalkeeper played really well.The player who stops the ball did a very good job.
  • They played badly in the first half.The team was poor in the first 45 minutes.
  • Our team is playing at home on Saturday.Our team has a match in our own stadium on Saturday.
  • Are you going to the game?Will you watch the match at the stadium?
  • I watched it on TV.I saw the match on television.
  • I think they'll win the league.In my opinion, they will be champions.
  • He's my favourite player.He is the player I like most.
  • Better luck next time!I hope your team does better in the next match.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hi! Did you watch the match last night?Hi! Did you see the game yesterday evening?
  • You: No, I missed it. What was the score?No, I didn't see it. What was the result?
  • Partner: It ended two–one. It was a great game!The final score was 2–1. It was a very good match!
  • You: Nice. Who scored?Good. Which players got the goals?
  • Partner: Our striker scored in the last minute. That was a penalty.Our forward got a goal at the end of the match. It was a penalty kick.
  • You: Wow! And who do you support?Wow! And which team do you follow?
  • Partner: I'm a big fan of City. They're playing at home on Saturday. Are you going?I really like and follow City. They have a match in their stadium on Saturday. Will you go?
  • You: I can't, but I'll watch it on TV. I support United.I can't go, but I will watch it on television. I follow United.
  • Partner: Oh no! Then better luck next time!Oh no! Then I hope your team does better in the next match!
  • You: Thanks. I think we'll win the league this year.Thanks. In my opinion, we will be champions this year.

Typical mistakes

I support to Liverpool.
I support Liverpool.
After 'support' we use the team name directly, without 'to'.
They won with three goals to zero.
They won three–nil.
In football we say the score simply, for example 'three–nil'.
The match was very funny.
The match was very exciting.
'Funny' means it made you laugh; for a good match, say 'exciting' or 'great'.

Tip

Learn the score in English: say 'two–one' or 'three–nil', not 'two to one'. Practise saying 'nil' for zero.
