A Person Who Inspired You in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you describe a person who inspired you in IELTS Speaking Part 2. You can also use them when the examiner asks follow-up questions about that person.

Useful phrases

  • I'd like to talk about my grandmother.I want to talk about my grandmother.
  • She has had a big influence on me.She has changed me in an important way.
  • She is a very kind and patient person.She is nice and calm with people.
  • She always helps other people.She often gives help to others.
  • She taught me to never give up.She showed me to keep trying.
  • She works hard every day.She does a lot of work each day.
  • When I was a child, she looked after me.She took care of me when I was young.
  • She supports me in everything I do.She helps and encourages me in all my activities.
  • Thanks to her, I became more confident.Because of her, I feel better about myself.
  • I really look up to her.I respect and admire her.
  • She is my role model.I want to be like her.
  • That's why she is so important to me.That is the reason she matters so much to me.
  • How long have you known this person?For what time have you known this person?
  • What exactly did this person do to help you?What did the person do to support you?
  • Can you give me an example?Can you tell me one specific case?
  • How did this person change your life?In what way is your life different because of this person?
  • Do you still keep in touch with this person?Do you still talk to this person now?
  • Would you like to be like this person?Do you want to be similar to this person?
  • Let me think for a second.Please give me a moment to think.
  • That's a good question. I'd say yes.That is a good question. I think the answer is yes.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Now I'd like you to describe a person who inspired you. You have one minute to prepare.Now please describe a person who inspired you. You have one minute to get ready.
  • You: OK, thank you. I'd like to talk about my grandmother.OK, thank you. I want to talk about my grandmother.
  • You: She has had a big influence on me. She is a very kind and patient person.She has changed me in an important way. She is a very nice and calm person.
  • You: When I was a child, she looked after me. She taught me to never give up.When I was young, she took care of me. She showed me to keep trying.
  • You: Thanks to her, I became more confident. I really look up to her.Because of her, I feel better about myself. I really respect and admire her.
  • Partner: Good. What exactly did she do to help you?Good. What did she do to support you?
  • You: She supports me in everything I do. For example, she helped me with my homework every evening.She helps and encourages me in all my activities. For example, she helped me with my homework every evening.
  • Partner: How did she change your life?In what way is your life different because of her?
  • You: She made me believe in myself. That's why she is so important to me.She made me feel sure about my abilities. That is the reason she matters so much to me.
  • Partner: Do you still keep in touch with her?Do you still talk to her now?
  • You: Yes, I do. I call her every week. She is my role model.Yes, I do. I phone her every week. I want to be like her.

Typical mistakes

She has influence on me since I was a child.
She has had an influence on me since I was a child.
With 'since', use the present perfect: 'has had'.
She is very much patient.
She is very patient.
'Very much' does not go before an adjective like 'patient'.
She taught me to don't give up.
She taught me not to give up.
After 'taught me' use 'not to' + verb, not 'don't'.

Tip

Choose one person you know well and prepare four small stories about them: one old memory, one recent example, one thing they taught you and one way you are similar. Short real stories are easier to remember than long descriptions.
