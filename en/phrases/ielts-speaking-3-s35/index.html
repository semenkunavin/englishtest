IELTS Speaking Part 3: technology in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about technology in IELTS Speaking Part 3. Use them to give your opinion and add clear reasons, even with simple English.

Useful phrases

  • In my opinion, ...In my opinion, ...
  • I think technology makes life easier.I think technology makes life easier.
  • For example, ...For example, ...
  • That's because ...That's because ...
  • On the one hand, ... On the other hand, ...On the one hand, ... On the other hand, ...
  • It depends on the person.It depends on the person.
  • I agree with that idea.I agree with that idea.
  • I'm not sure about that.I'm not sure about that.
  • In my experience, ...In my experience, ...
  • Most people I know ...Most people I know ...
  • One good thing is that ...One good thing is that ...
  • One bad thing is that ...One bad thing is that ...
  • It saves time.It saves time.
  • It can be a problem for older people.It can be a problem for older people.
  • People spend too much time on their phones.People spend too much time on their phones.
  • Can you say more about that?Can you say more about that?
  • That's a good point.That's a good point.
  • Let me think for a second.Let me think for a second.
  • To sum up, ...To sum up, ...
  • Overall, I think ...Overall, I think ...

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: How has technology changed the way people talk to each other?How has technology changed the way people talk to each other?
  • You: In my opinion, it has made talking much faster. For example, I can send a message to my family in seconds.In my opinion, it has made talking much faster. For example, I can send a message to my family in seconds.
  • Partner: Do you think that is always a good thing?Do you think that is always a good thing?
  • You: Not always. One bad thing is that people spend too much time on their phones and talk less face to face.Not always. One bad thing is that people spend too much time on their phones and talk less face to face.
  • Partner: Why do you think some people prefer messages to phone calls?Why do you think some people prefer messages to phone calls?
  • You: That's because messages are quick and quiet. In my experience, young people feel shy on the phone.That's because messages are quick and quiet. In my experience, young people feel shy on the phone.
  • Partner: Is technology good for older people too?Is technology good for older people too?
  • You: It depends on the person. One good thing is that video calls help them see family far away. But some find new apps hard to use.It depends on the person. One good thing is that video calls help them see family far away. But some find new apps hard to use.
  • Partner: So, overall, is technology good or bad for communication?So, overall, is technology good or bad for communication?
  • You: Overall, I think it is good, but we need to use it carefully. To sum up, it saves time, but we should still talk in person.Overall, I think it is good, but we need to use it carefully. To sum up, it saves time, but we should still talk in person.

Typical mistakes

I think technology is good because it is good.
I think technology is good because it saves time.
Give a real reason, not the same idea again.
Example, my phone.
For example, I use my phone to talk to my family.
Say 'For example' and finish with a full sentence.
People is spending too much time online.
People are spending too much time online.
'People' is plural, so use 'are'.

Tip

Give one opinion, then one simple example, then a short reason. This keeps your answer clear and easy to follow.
