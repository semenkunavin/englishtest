Taking a Taxi in English: Phrases and a Sample Dialogue

These phrases will help you when you take a taxi in an English-speaking country. You will learn how to give the address, ask about the price, and make small talk during the ride.

Useful phrases

  • Where to?Where do you want to go?
  • Can you take me to this address, please?Please drive me to this address.
  • How much will it cost to go to the airport?What is the price to the airport?
  • Could you turn on the meter, please?Please start the meter.
  • How long will the ride take?What is the duration of the trip?
  • Please stop here.I want to get out here.
  • Can you wait for me here? I'll be back in five minutes.Please wait for me here; I will return soon.
  • How much is the fare?What is the price of the ride?
  • Can I pay by credit card?Is it possible to pay with a credit card?
  • Keep the change.You can keep the extra money.
  • Could you help me with my luggage?Please assist me with my bags.
  • Is it a long ride?Will the trip take a lot of time?
  • I'm in a hurry, please.I need to get there quickly.
  • Is there a lot of traffic?Are there many cars on the road?
  • Can you open the window, please?Please let some air in.
  • Could you turn down the music, please?Please make the music quieter.
  • What's the best way to get there?What route do you recommend?
  • Are you free?Are you available for a ride?
  • Here is the address.This is the location I want to go to.
  • Thank you, have a nice day!Thank you, I hope your day is good.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Good morning! Where to?Good morning! Where do you want to go?
  • You: Hi! Can you take me to 123 Main Street, please?Hello! Please drive me to 123 Main Street.
  • Partner: Sure. Do you know how to get there?Of course. Do you know the way?
  • You: I think so, but please use the GPS. How much will it cost?I think so, but please use the GPS. What is the price?
  • Partner: It should be about $15. The meter will show the exact price.It will be around $15. The meter will display the exact amount.
  • You: Okay. How long will the ride take?Alright. How long will the trip take?
  • Partner: About 20 minutes if there's no traffic.Around 20 minutes if the roads are clear.
  • You: Great. I'm in a bit of a hurry, so please take the fastest route.Good. I need to be quick, so please choose the quickest way.
  • Partner: No problem. Is the temperature okay?That's fine. Is the temperature comfortable?
  • You: Yes, it's fine. Thanks.Yes, it's good. Thank you.
  • Partner: Here we are. That's $15.50.We have arrived. The fare is $15.50.
  • You: Here's $20. Keep the change.Here is $20. You can keep the extra.

Typical mistakes

I want go to the airport.
I want to go to the airport.
Use 'to' before the verb 'go'.
How much cost?
How much does it cost?
Use 'does' to form a question in the present simple.
Please stop the car here.
Please stop here, driver.
'Stop the car' is not wrong, but 'stop here, driver' is more natural when speaking to a taxi driver.

Tip

Always check that the driver uses the meter or agree on the price before you start the ride to avoid surprises.
