Shopping for Clothes in English: Phrases and a Sample Dialogue

These phrases will help you when you go shopping for clothes in an English-speaking country. They cover asking for sizes, colours, trying things on, and returning items.

Useful phrases

  • Can I help you?Can I help you?
  • I'm just looking, thanks.I'm just looking, thanks.
  • Do you have this in a medium?Do you have this in a medium?
  • What size are you?What size are you?
  • I'm looking for a blue shirt.I'm looking for a blue shirt.
  • Do you have this in another colour?Do you have this in another colour?
  • Can I try this on?Can I try this on?
  • The fitting rooms are over there.The fitting rooms are over there.
  • How does it fit?How does it fit?
  • It's too tight.It's too tight.
  • It's too loose.It's too loose.
  • Do you have a bigger size?Do you have a bigger size?
  • Do you have a smaller size?Do you have a smaller size?
  • How much is it?How much is it?
  • It's on sale.It's on sale.
  • I'll take it.I'll take it.
  • Can I pay by card?Can I pay by card?
  • I'd like to return this, please.I'd like to return this, please.
  • Do you have the receipt?Do you have the receipt?
  • Can I get a refund?Can I get a refund?

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hello! Can I help you?Hello! Can I help you?
  • You: Yes, I'm looking for a blue shirt.Yes, I'm looking for a blue shirt.
  • Partner: What size are you?What size are you?
  • You: I'm a medium.I'm a medium.
  • Partner: Here you are. The fitting rooms are over there.Here you are. The fitting rooms are over there.
  • You: Thanks. ... It's too tight. Do you have a large?Thanks. ... It's too tight. Do you have a large?
  • Partner: Yes, here's a large. How does it fit?Yes, here's a large. How does it fit?
  • You: It fits well. How much is it?It fits well. How much is it?
  • Partner: It's $25. It's on sale.It's $25. It's on sale.
  • You: Great, I'll take it. Can I pay by card?Great, I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
  • Partner: Yes, of course. Here's your receipt.Yes, of course. Here's your receipt.
  • You: Thank you.Thank you.

Typical mistakes

I want to try this clothes.
I want to try these clothes on.
'Clothes' is plural, so use 'these'. Also, 'try on' is the phrasal verb.
Do you have this in size more big?
Do you have this in a bigger size?
Use 'bigger' before 'size', not 'more big'.
I like to return this shirt.
I'd like to return this shirt.
Use 'I'd like to' for polite requests, not 'I like to'.

Tip

Always check the return policy before you buy: ask 'Can I return this if it doesn't fit?' and keep your receipt.
