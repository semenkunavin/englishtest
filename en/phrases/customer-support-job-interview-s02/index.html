Customer Support Job Interview in English: Phrases and Sample Dialogue

These phrases help you in a job interview for a customer support role. Use them to answer questions clearly and show you are good with people.

Useful phrases

  • Why do you want to work in customer support?Why do you want to work in customer support?
  • I enjoy helping people solve problems.I enjoy helping people solve problems.
  • I am patient and a good listener.I am patient and a good listener.
  • How would you calm an angry customer?How would you calm an angry customer?
  • I would listen carefully and stay calm.I would listen carefully and stay calm.
  • I would say, 'I understand why you are upset.'I would say, 'I understand why you are upset.'
  • Are you okay with working shifts?Are you okay with working shifts?
  • Yes, I am flexible with working hours.Yes, I am flexible with working hours.
  • Can you tell me about a difficult customer you helped?Can you tell me about a difficult customer you helped?
  • Sure. Once, a customer was angry about a late delivery.Sure. Once, a customer was angry about a late delivery.
  • I apologized and explained the situation.I apologized and explained the situation.
  • What do you do if you don't know the answer?What do you do if you don't know the answer?
  • I would ask a colleague or a supervisor.I would ask a colleague or a supervisor.
  • How do you handle stress?How do you handle stress?
  • I take a deep breath and focus on one thing at a time.I take a deep breath and focus on one thing at a time.
  • Do you have any questions for us?Do you have any questions for us?
  • Yes, what training do you provide?Yes, what training do you provide?
  • Thank you for your time.Thank you for your time.
  • I am looking forward to hearing from you.I am looking forward to hearing from you.
  • I am good at staying calm under pressure.I am good at staying calm under pressure.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Good morning. Please have a seat. Why do you want to work in customer support?Good morning. Please have a seat. Why do you want to work in customer support?
  • You: Good morning. I enjoy helping people solve problems. I am patient and a good listener.Good morning. I enjoy helping people solve problems. I am patient and a good listener.
  • Partner: Great. How would you calm an angry customer?Great. How would you calm an angry customer?
  • You: I would listen carefully and stay calm. I would say, 'I understand why you are upset. Let me help you.'I would listen carefully and stay calm. I would say, 'I understand why you are upset. Let me help you.'
  • Partner: Are you okay with working shifts, including evenings and weekends?Are you okay with working shifts, including evenings and weekends?
  • You: Yes, I am flexible with working hours. I can work shifts.Yes, I am flexible with working hours. I can work shifts.
  • Partner: Can you tell me about a difficult customer you helped?Can you tell me about a difficult customer you helped?
  • You: Sure. Once, a customer was angry about a late delivery. I apologized and explained the situation. I stayed calm and helped him track his order.Sure. Once, a customer was angry about a late delivery. I apologized and explained the situation. I stayed calm and helped him track his order.
  • Partner: What do you do if you don't know the answer to a customer's question?What do you do if you don't know the answer to a customer's question?
  • You: I would ask a colleague or a supervisor. I want to give the customer the right information.I would ask a colleague or a supervisor. I want to give the customer the right information.
  • Partner: Do you have any questions for us?Do you have any questions for us?
  • You: Yes, what training do you provide? Thank you for your time. I am looking forward to hearing from you.Yes, what training do you provide? Thank you for your time. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Typical mistakes

I am interesting in this job.
I am interested in this job.
Use 'interested' to describe how you feel, not 'interesting'.
I can explain you the problem.
I can explain the problem to you.
We explain something to someone, not explain someone something.
I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
'Agree' is a verb, so do not use 'am' before it.

Tip

Practice your answers out loud before the interview. Speak slowly and clearly, and give short examples from your experience.
