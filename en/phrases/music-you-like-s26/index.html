Music you like in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about music you enjoy, concerts you go to, and instruments you play. Use them in everyday conversations with friends or when you meet new people.

Useful phrases

  • What kind of music do you like?What kind of music do you like?
  • I'm really into pop music.I'm really into pop music.
  • I love listening to rock songs.I love listening to rock songs.
  • My favorite band is Coldplay.My favorite band is Coldplay.
  • I don't like heavy metal much.I don't like heavy metal much.
  • Do you play any instruments?Do you play any instruments?
  • I play the guitar a little.I play the guitar a little.
  • I'm learning to play the piano.I'm learning to play the piano.
  • Have you ever been to a concert?Have you ever been to a concert?
  • I went to a concert last weekend.I went to a concert last weekend.
  • The concert was amazing!The concert was amazing!
  • Who is your favorite singer?Who is your favorite singer?
  • I like songs that have good lyrics.I like songs that have good lyrics.
  • I usually listen to music on my phone.I usually listen to music on my phone.
  • What was the last concert you went to?What was the last concert you went to?
  • I'd love to see them live.I'd love to see them live.
  • Do you prefer live music or recorded music?Do you prefer live music or recorded music?
  • I'm not a big fan of classical music.I'm not a big fan of classical music.
  • That song is really catchy.That song is really catchy.
  • I can't stop listening to this album.I can't stop listening to this album.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hey, what kind of music do you like?Hey, what kind of music do you like?
  • You: I'm really into rock and pop. What about you?I'm really into rock and pop. What about you?
  • Partner: I like jazz and blues. Do you play any instruments?I like jazz and blues. Do you play any instruments?
  • You: I play the guitar a little. I'm learning to play the piano too.I play the guitar a little. I'm learning to play the piano too.
  • Partner: That's cool! Have you ever been to a concert?That's cool! Have you ever been to a concert?
  • You: Yes, I went to a rock concert last month. It was amazing!Yes, I went to a rock concert last month. It was amazing!
  • Partner: Who was playing?Who was playing?
  • You: A local band. I'd love to see them live again.A local band. I'd love to see them live again.
  • Partner: Do you prefer live music or recorded music?Do you prefer live music or recorded music?
  • You: I prefer live music because the energy is great. But I usually listen to music on my phone.I prefer live music because the energy is great. But I usually listen to music on my phone.

Typical mistakes

I like listen to music.
I like listening to music.
After 'like', use the -ing form when talking about enjoyment.
I play guitar.
I play the guitar.
We usually say 'play the guitar' (with 'the') when talking about instruments.
I have been to a concert last week.
I went to a concert last week.
Use the past simple with a finished time expression like 'last week'.

Tip

When you talk about music, try to give reasons. For example, say 'I like pop because it's catchy' instead of just 'I like pop'. This makes the conversation more interesting.
