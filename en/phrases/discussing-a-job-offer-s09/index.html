Discussing a Job Offer in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you when an HR manager offers you a job and you need to talk about pay, hours and your first day. You can use them to ask questions, say thank you, and negotiate politely.

Useful phrases

  • Thank you for the offer. I'm really happy to hear that.Thank you for the offer. I'm really happy to hear that.
  • Could you tell me a bit more about the salary?Could you tell me a bit more about the salary?
  • What is the monthly salary, before tax?What is the monthly salary, before tax?
  • Is the salary fixed, or can we talk about it?Is the salary fixed, or can we talk about it?
  • I was hoping for a little more, around ...I was hoping for a little more, around ...
  • Would it be possible to get ... ?Would it be possible to get ... ?
  • That sounds fair to me.That sounds fair to me.
  • Let me think about it and get back to you.Let me think about it and get back to you.
  • What are the working hours?What are the working hours?
  • Is the job full-time or part-time?Is the job full-time or part-time?
  • Can I work from home some days?Can I work from home some days?
  • Is there any weekend or evening work?Is there any weekend or evening work?
  • When would you like me to start?When would you like me to start?
  • I can start on the first of next month.I can start on the first of next month.
  • I need to give my current job two weeks' notice.I need to give my current job two weeks' notice.
  • Would the start date be flexible?Would the start date be flexible?
  • Sorry, could you say that again, please?Sorry, could you say that again, please?
  • I have one more question, if that's OK.I have one more question, if that's OK.
  • Thank you. I'm very interested in the job.Thank you. I'm very interested in the job.
  • I'll send you my answer by email tomorrow.I'll send you my answer by email tomorrow.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hello, this is Anna from HR. We'd like to offer you the job.Hello, this is Anna from HR. We'd like to offer you the job.
  • You: Thank you! I'm really happy to hear that.Thank you! I'm really happy to hear that.
  • Partner: The salary is 2,000 euros a month, before tax. It's a full-time job, 9 to 5.The salary is 2,000 euros a month, before tax. It's a full-time job, 9 to 5.
  • You: Thanks for the details. Is the salary fixed, or can we talk about it?Thanks for the details. Is the salary fixed, or can we talk about it?
  • Partner: We can talk about it. What did you have in mind?We can talk about it. What did you have in mind?
  • You: I was hoping for around 2,200. Would that be possible?I was hoping for around 2,200. Would that be possible?
  • Partner: I think we can do 2,100. Does that work for you?I think we can do 2,100. Does that work for you?
  • You: Yes, that sounds fair to me. And when would you like me to start?Yes, that sounds fair to me. And when would you like me to start?
  • Partner: Could you start on the first of next month?Could you start on the first of next month?
  • You: I need to give my current job two weeks' notice, so the tenth would be better for me.I need to give my current job two weeks' notice, so the tenth would be better for me.
  • Partner: The tenth is fine. We'll send you the contract today.The tenth is fine. We'll send you the contract today.
  • You: Great, thank you. I'll read it and reply by email tomorrow.Great, thank you. I'll read it and reply by email tomorrow.

Typical mistakes

I want more money.
I was hoping for a little more, around 2,200.
"I want" sounds strong and rude. "I was hoping for..." is polite and soft.
How much is the salary cost?
What is the salary?
"How much is the salary cost" uses the wrong words. Ask "What is the salary?" or "Could you tell me the salary?"
I start tomorrow, no problem.
I can start next Monday, if that works for you.
Check the date with the company. "If that works for you" is polite and leaves room to agree.

Tip

Say thank you first, then ask one question at a time. It is fine to say "Let me think about it and get back to you" — you do not have to say yes on the phone.
