Telling a story in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you tell friends about things that happened in the past, like your last weekend. Use them when someone asks "What did you do at the weekend?" and you want to tell a short story.

Useful phrases

  • How was your weekend?How was your weekend?
  • What did you get up to?What did you get up to?
  • Did you do anything nice?Did you do anything nice?
  • Tell me everything!Tell me everything!
  • It was great, thanks.It was great, thanks.
  • It was pretty quiet, actually.It was pretty quiet, actually.
  • On Saturday morning, I went for a run.On Saturday morning, I went for a run.
  • In the afternoon, we met some friends.In the afternoon, we met some friends.
  • In the evening, I stayed in and watched a film.In the evening, I stayed in and watched a film.
  • On Sunday, I had a lazy day.On Sunday, I had a lazy day.
  • We had lunch in a little café.We had lunch in a little café.
  • Then we walked around the park.Then we walked around the park.
  • After that, I went home.After that, I went home.
  • Later, I called my sister.Later, I called my sister.
  • It was really fun.It was really fun.
  • It was a bit boring, to be honest.It was a bit boring, to be honest.
  • The weather was lovely.The weather was lovely.
  • We were so tired after that.We were so tired after that.
  • I didn't do much.I didn't do much.
  • What about you? What did you do?What about you? What did you do?

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hey! How was your weekend?Hey! How was your weekend?
  • You: It was great, thanks. Really busy.It was great, thanks. Really busy.
  • Partner: Nice! What did you get up to?Nice! What did you get up to?
  • You: On Saturday morning, I went for a run in the park.On Saturday morning, I went for a run in the park.
  • Partner: Good for you! And later?Good for you! And later?
  • You: In the afternoon, I met some friends and we had lunch in a little café.In the afternoon, I met some friends and we had lunch in a little café.
  • Partner: That sounds nice. What did you do in the evening?That sounds nice. What did you do in the evening?
  • You: I stayed in and watched a film. I was so tired!I stayed in and watched a film. I was so tired!
  • Partner: And Sunday?And Sunday?
  • You: I had a lazy day. I didn't do much, just called my sister. What about you?I had a lazy day. I didn't do much, just called my sister. What about you?
  • Partner: I went hiking with my family. It was really fun.I went hiking with my family. It was really fun.

Typical mistakes

Yesterday I go to the cinema.
Yesterday I went to the cinema.
Use the past form 'went' for finished actions in the past.
I didn't went out on Saturday.
I didn't go out on Saturday.
After 'didn't', use the base form of the verb, not the past form.
On Sunday I have a lazy day.
On Sunday I had a lazy day.
'Have' becomes 'had' in the past.

Tip

Tell your story in order with simple time phrases: on Saturday morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, then, after that, later. This makes your weekend story easy to follow.
