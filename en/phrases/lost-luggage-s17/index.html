Lost luggage in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you when your suitcase does not arrive at the airport. You can describe your bag and give your details for the claim.

Useful phrases

  • My suitcase didn't arrive.My suitcase didn't arrive.
  • My bag is missing from the belt.My bag is missing from the belt.
  • I can't find my luggage.I can't find my luggage.
  • Here is my baggage tag.Here is my baggage tag.
  • My flight number is BA 245.My flight number is BA 245.
  • It's a large black suitcase.It's a large black suitcase.
  • It has a red ribbon on the handle.It has a red ribbon on the handle.
  • It's a soft bag with wheels.It's a soft bag with wheels.
  • There's a name tag with my address.There's a name tag with my address.
  • It weighs about 20 kilos.It weighs about 20 kilos.
  • Can you check where my bag is?Can you check where my bag is?
  • When will you know something?When will you know something?
  • Where can I pick it up?Where can I pick it up?
  • Can you deliver it to my hotel?Can you deliver it to my hotel?
  • What's your full name?What's your full name?
  • Can I have your phone number?Can I have your phone number?
  • What's your address in this country?What's your address in this country?
  • Please fill in this form.Please fill in this form.
  • We'll call you when we find it.We'll call you when we find it.
  • Here's your claim number.Here's your claim number.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hello. How can I help you?Hello. How can I help you?
  • You: Hi. My suitcase didn't arrive. I waited at the belt for thirty minutes.Hi. My suitcase didn't arrive. I waited at the belt for thirty minutes.
  • Partner: I'm sorry about that. Can I see your baggage tag and boarding pass?I'm sorry about that. Can I see your baggage tag and boarding pass?
  • You: Sure. Here they are. My flight was BA 245 from London.Sure. Here they are. My flight was BA 245 from London.
  • Partner: Thank you. What does your suitcase look like?Thank you. What does your suitcase look like?
  • You: It's a large black suitcase with wheels. It has a red ribbon on the handle.It's a large black suitcase with wheels. It has a red ribbon on the handle.
  • Partner: OK. Can I have your name, phone number, and address here?OK. Can I have your name, phone number, and address here?
  • You: Yes. I'm Anna Smith. My number is 07700 900123. I'm staying at the Park Hotel.Yes. I'm Anna Smith. My number is 07700 900123. I'm staying at the Park Hotel.
  • Partner: Thanks. We'll call you when we find it. Here's your claim number.Thanks. We'll call you when we find it. Here's your claim number.
  • You: Thank you. Can you deliver it to my hotel?Thank you. Can you deliver it to my hotel?
  • Partner: Yes, of course. Sorry for the trouble.Yes, of course. Sorry for the trouble.

Typical mistakes

My luggage didn't arrived.
My luggage didn't arrive.
After didn't, use the base verb, not the past form.
I am looking for my bag since one hour.
I have been looking for my bag for an hour.
Use for with a length of time, and the present perfect for something continuing.
It is a black large suitcase.
It's a large black suitcase.
Put size before colour in English.

Tip

Take a photo of your suitcase before you fly and keep your baggage tag. A photo helps you describe the bag and shows it is yours.
