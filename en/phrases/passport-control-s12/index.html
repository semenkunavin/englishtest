Passport control in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you answer the border officer at passport control in an airport. They cover the three usual questions: why you are visiting, how long you will stay, and where you will stay.

Useful phrases

  • Good morning. Here is my passport.Good morning. Here is my passport.
  • What is the purpose of your visit?What is the purpose of your visit?
  • I'm here on holiday.I'm here on holiday.
  • I'm here to study English.I'm here to study English.
  • I'm here on business.I'm here on business.
  • I'm visiting my family.I'm visiting my family.
  • I'm a student.I'm a student.
  • How long are you staying?How long are you staying?
  • I'm staying for two weeks.I'm staying for two weeks.
  • I'm staying for three months.I'm staying for three months.
  • I'm flying home on 15 July.I'm flying home on 15 July.
  • Where are you staying?Where are you staying?
  • I'm staying at a hotel in the city centre.I'm staying at a hotel in the city centre.
  • I'm staying with a host family.I'm staying with a host family.
  • I'm staying in a student residence.I'm staying in a student residence.
  • Here is my booking confirmation.Here is my booking confirmation.
  • Do you have your return ticket?Do you have your return ticket?
  • Yes, it's on my phone.Yes, it's on my phone.
  • Is this your first visit to the UK?Is this your first visit to the UK?
  • Thank you. Have a nice stay.Thank you. Have a nice stay.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Good morning. Passport, please.Good morning. Passport, please.
  • You: Good morning. Here it is.Good morning. Here it is.
  • Partner: What is the purpose of your visit?What is the purpose of your visit?
  • You: I'm here to study English at a language school.I'm here to study English at a language school.
  • Partner: How long are you staying?How long are you staying?
  • You: I'm staying for three weeks. I fly home on 20 August.I'm staying for three weeks. I fly home on 20 August.
  • Partner: Where are you staying?Where are you staying?
  • You: I'm staying with a host family in Brighton. Here is the address.I'm staying with a host family in Brighton. Here is the address.
  • Partner: Do you have a return ticket?Do you have a return ticket?
  • You: Yes, it's on my phone.Yes, it's on my phone.
  • Partner: Thank you. Enjoy your stay.Thank you. Enjoy your stay.
  • You: Thank you very much.Thank you very much.

Typical mistakes

I stay three weeks.
I'm staying for three weeks.
Use the present continuous for a plan, and add 'for' before a length of time.
I go to study here.
I'm here to study English.
'I'm here to...' is the natural answer to 'What is the purpose of your visit?'
I live in a hotel.
I'm staying at a hotel.
For a short visit, say 'I'm staying at'; 'I live' means your permanent home.

Tip

Keep your answers short and clear, and have your passport, booking confirmation and return ticket ready.
