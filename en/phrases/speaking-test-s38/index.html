Free time in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you answer simple speaking test questions about your daily routine and free time. Use them to give short, clear answers and to keep the conversation going.

  • On weekdays, I...On weekdays, I...
  • At the weekend, I...At the weekend, I...

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: What do you usually do in your free time?What do you usually do in your free time?
  • You: In my free time, I usually hang out with my friends or play football.In my free time, I usually hang out with my friends or play football.
  • Partner: How often do you play football?How often do you play football?
  • You: I play twice a week, usually at the weekend.I play twice a week, usually at the weekend.
  • Partner: Do you prefer playing sports or watching them?Do you prefer playing sports or watching them?
  • You: I prefer playing. It helps me relax and I'm quite good at it.I prefer playing. It helps me relax and I'm quite good at it.
  • Partner: What about your daily routine? When do you get up?What about your daily routine? When do you get up?
  • You: On weekdays, I get up at seven and go to school by bus.On weekdays, I get up at seven and go to school by bus.
  • Partner: And what do you do after school?And what do you do after school?
  • You: After school, I do my homework, then I watch TV or read.After school, I do my homework, then I watch TV or read.
  • Partner: Thanks, that's all.Thanks, that's all.

Typical mistakes

I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
Use 'agree' without 'am'.
I go to football in the weekend.
I play football at the weekend.
We say 'play football' and 'at the weekend'.
I like to hang out with my friends very much always.
I really like hanging out with my friends.
Keep the word order simple: 'really like + -ing'.

Tip

Give a short answer, then add one extra sentence with a reason or an example — it shows the examiner you can say more.
