Airport Check-in in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you check in for a flight, hand over your passport and talk about your bags and seat. Use them at the check-in desk before security.

Useful phrases

  • Good morning. Here is my passport.Good morning. Here is my passport.
  • I'm flying to London today.I'm flying to London today.
  • I have one bag to check in.I have one bag to check in.
  • Can I take this bag on the plane?Can I take this bag on the plane?
  • I'd like a window seat, please.I'd like a window seat, please.
  • Could I have an aisle seat, please?Could I have an aisle seat, please?
  • How many bags are you checking in?How many bags are you checking in?
  • Please put your bag on the scale.Please put your bag on the scale.
  • Is this your only bag?Is this your only bag?
  • Your bag is two kilos too heavy.Your bag is two kilos too heavy.
  • Do you have any hand luggage?Do you have any hand luggage?
  • Here is your boarding pass.Here is your boarding pass.
  • Your gate is B12.Your gate is B12.
  • Boarding starts at 10:30.Boarding starts at 10:30.
  • What time does boarding start?What time does boarding start?
  • Which gate is it?Which gate is it?
  • Could you repeat that, please?Could you repeat that, please?
  • Sorry, I don't understand.Sorry, I don't understand.
  • Do you have a seat preference?Do you have a seat preference?
  • Have a good flight!Have a good flight!

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Good morning. Where are you flying today?Good morning. Where are you flying today?
  • You: Good morning. I'm flying to London.Good morning. I'm flying to London.
  • Partner: May I see your passport, please?May I see your passport, please?
  • You: Yes, here you are.Yes, here you are.
  • Partner: Thank you. Are you checking in any bags?Thank you. Are you checking in any bags?
  • You: Yes, one bag. And I have this small bag with me.Yes, one bag. And I have this small bag with me.
  • Partner: OK. Please put your bag on the scale. Do you have a seat preference?OK. Please put your bag on the scale. Do you have a seat preference?
  • You: Yes, I'd like a window seat, please.Yes, I'd like a window seat, please.
  • Partner: Here is your boarding pass. Your gate is B12 and boarding starts at 10:30.Here is your boarding pass. Your gate is B12 and boarding starts at 10:30.
  • You: Thank you. Have a nice day!Thank you. Have a nice day!

Typical mistakes

I have one luggage.
I have one bag.
Luggage is uncountable, so we say 'a bag' or 'some luggage'.
I want window seat.
I'd like a window seat, please.
Use 'a' before a singular noun and 'I'd like' for polite requests.
Where is my gate number?
Which gate is it?
'Which gate is it?' is the natural question at the check-in desk.

Tip

Keep your passport and boarding pass in your hand until you go through security. At the desk, speak slowly and say 'please' and 'thank you' — it makes everything easier.
