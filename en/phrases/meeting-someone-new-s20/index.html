Meeting Someone New in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you start a friendly conversation with someone you do not know, for example at a party, a class or a new job. They are short, polite and easy to remember, so you can use them right away.

  • Partner: Hi! I'm Sam. Nice to meet you.Hi! I'm Sam. Nice to meet you.

Typical mistakes

I am Anna. How are you called?
I'm Anna. What's your name?
In English we say 'What's your name?', not 'How are you called?'
Where you are from?
Where are you from?
In a question, put 'are' before 'you'.
I have 25 years.
I'm 25 years old.
For age, use 'I'm' + number + 'years old'.

Tip

After you say your name, ask the other person a question like 'And you?' or 'Where are you from?'. This keeps the conversation going and shows you are friendly.
