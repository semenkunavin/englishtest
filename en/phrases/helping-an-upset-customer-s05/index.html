A Broken Order in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you stay calm and polite when a customer is angry about a broken order. You can use them on the phone, in chat, or by email.

Useful phrases

  • I'm really sorry about that.I'm really sorry about that.
  • I understand why you're upset.I understand why you're upset.
  • That must be very frustrating.That must be very frustrating.
  • Thank you for telling us.Thank you for telling us.
  • Let me help you with this.Let me help you with this.
  • Can I have your order number, please?Can I have your order number, please?
  • Could you send me a photo of the damage?Could you send me a photo of the damage?
  • I'll check this right away.I'll check this right away.
  • Let me look into it for you.Let me look into it for you.
  • We can send you a new one.We can send you a new one.
  • Would you like a replacement or a refund?Would you like a replacement or a refund?
  • I'll send you a prepaid return label.I'll send you a prepaid return label.
  • The refund will be back on your card in 3–5 days.The refund will be back on your card in 3–5 days.
  • I'll keep you updated by email.I'll keep you updated by email.
  • Is there anything else I can do for you?Is there anything else I can do for you?
  • Thanks for your patience.Thanks for your patience.
  • My order arrived broken.My order arrived broken.

  • Partner: Hello, my order just arrived and the box was completely crushed. The lamp inside is broken.Hello, my order just arrived and the box was completely crushed. The lamp inside is broken.

  • Partner: Yes, it's 4-8-2-1-6. I paid extra for safe packing, so this is really disappointing.Yes, it's 4-8-2-1-6. I paid extra for safe packing, so this is really disappointing.

  • Partner: That sounds good. Thank you for sorting it out so quickly.That sounds good. Thank you for sorting it out so quickly.

Typical mistakes

I am sorry for you.
I'm sorry about that.
"Sorry for you" means you feel pity for someone. Use "sorry about" for a problem.
Wait a moment, I check your order.
Please wait a moment while I check your order.
Add "please" and use "while I check" to sound polite and natural.
You must send me a photo.
Could you send me a photo, please?
"Must" sounds like an order. Use "Could you...?" with an upset customer.

Tip

Let the customer finish speaking before you reply, and repeat the key facts back to them (order number, what is broken, what you will do). This shows you are listening and helps you stay calm.
