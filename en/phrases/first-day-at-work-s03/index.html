First Day at Work in English: Phrases and a Sample Dialogue

These phrases help you on your first day at a new job. You can use them to introduce yourself, ask where things are, and ask for help.

Useful phrases

  • Hi, I'm [name]. I'm new here.Hi, I'm [name]. I'm new here.
  • Nice to meet you.Nice to meet you.
  • What's your name?What's your name?
  • I'm in the marketing team.I'm in the marketing team.
  • Where is the bathroom?Where is the bathroom?
  • Where is the kitchen?Where is the kitchen?
  • Where can I get a coffee?Where can I get a coffee?
  • Where is my desk?Where is my desk?
  • Can you show me where the printer is?Can you show me where the printer is?
  • I don't know where the meeting room is.I don't know where the meeting room is.
  • Can you help me, please?Can you help me, please?
  • I have a question.I have a question.
  • How do I use the coffee machine?How do I use the coffee machine?
  • How do I log in to my computer?How do I log in to my computer?
  • Where can I find the files?Where can I find the files?
  • Who should I ask about this?Who should I ask about this?
  • Thanks for your help.Thanks for your help.
  • It's my first day, so I'm still learning.It's my first day, so I'm still learning.
  • Can you say that again, please?Can you say that again, please?
  • I'm sorry, I don't understand.I'm sorry, I don't understand.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hi! You must be the new person. I'm Alex.Hi! You must be the new person. I'm Alex.
  • You: Hi, Alex. I'm Sam. Nice to meet you.Hi, Alex. I'm Sam. Nice to meet you.
  • Partner: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?
  • You: I'm from Spain. It's my first day here.I'm from Spain. It's my first day here.
  • Partner: Welcome! Let me show you around. Your desk is over there.Welcome! Let me show you around. Your desk is over there.
  • You: Thanks. Where is the kitchen?Thanks. Where is the kitchen?
  • Partner: It's next to the meeting room. Can you see the door?It's next to the meeting room. Can you see the door?
  • You: Yes, I see it. And where is the bathroom?Yes, I see it. And where is the bathroom?
  • Partner: It's down the hall on the left.It's down the hall on the left.
  • You: Great. One more question: how do I log in to my computer?Great. One more question: how do I log in to my computer?
  • Partner: I'll show you. It's easy. Just use your email and password.I'll show you. It's easy. Just use your email and password.
  • You: Thanks for your help!Thanks for your help!

Typical mistakes

I am new here. Where is the toilet?
I'm new here. Where is the bathroom?
In a work situation, 'bathroom' is more polite and common than 'toilet' in English.
Can you help me?
Can you help me, please?
Adding 'please' makes your request more polite.
I don't understand. Repeat, please.
I don't understand. Can you say that again, please?
'Repeat' alone sounds like an order. Use 'Can you say that again, please?' to be polite.

Tip

Smile and say 'Nice to meet you' when you meet someone new. It's a simple and friendly way to start.
