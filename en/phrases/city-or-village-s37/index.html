City or village? in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you give your opinion about living in a city or a village and explain your reasons. Use them in conversations, class discussions, or speaking exams.

Useful phrases

  • In my opinion, living in a city is better.In my opinion, living in a city is better.
  • I think a village is a nicer place to live.I think a village is a nicer place to live.
  • For me, the city is too noisy.For me, the city is too noisy.
  • I prefer the city because there are more jobs.I prefer the city because there are more jobs.
  • One good thing about the village is the fresh air.One good thing about the village is the fresh air.
  • The main reason is that life is quieter there.The main reason is that life is quieter there.
  • I'm not sure. I can see both sides.I'm not sure. I can see both sides.
  • What do you think about living in a village?What do you think about living in a village?
  • Do you agree that cities are too busy?Do you agree that cities are too busy?
  • That's a good point, but I still prefer the city.That's a good point, but I still prefer the city.
  • I see what you mean, but I don't agree.I see what you mean, but I don't agree.
  • On the one hand, it's quiet. On the other hand, it's far from everything.On the one hand, it's quiet. On the other hand, it's far from everything.
  • For example, there are more shops and schools in the city.For example, there are more shops and schools in the city.
  • In the village, everyone knows each other.In the village, everyone knows each other.
  • It's easier to meet people in the city.It's easier to meet people in the city.
  • The traffic in the city is a big problem.The traffic in the city is a big problem.
  • Villages are cheaper, but there are fewer jobs.Villages are cheaper, but there are fewer jobs.
  • To be honest, I'd like to live in a small town.To be honest, I'd like to live in a small town.
  • Can you give me an example?Can you give me an example?
  • So, all in all, I think the village is better for families.So, all in all, I think the village is better for families.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Do you think it's better to live in a city or in a village?Do you think it's better to live in a city or in a village?
  • You: In my opinion, a village is better for me.In my opinion, a village is better for me.
  • Partner: Why do you think that?Why do you think that?
  • You: The main reason is that life is quieter and the air is fresh.The main reason is that life is quieter and the air is fresh.
  • Partner: That's true, but what about jobs and schools?That's true, but what about jobs and schools?
  • You: I see what you mean. Cities have more jobs, but they are also very busy.I see what you mean. Cities have more jobs, but they are also very busy.
  • Partner: So would you live in a village all your life?So would you live in a village all your life?
  • You: Maybe not. To be honest, I'd like to live in a small town near a city.Maybe not. To be honest, I'd like to live in a small town near a city.
  • Partner: That sounds like a good idea. Can you give me an example?That sounds like a good idea. Can you give me an example?
  • You: Yes. A small town is quiet, but the city is only thirty minutes away.Yes. A small town is quiet, but the city is only thirty minutes away.
  • Partner: So, all in all, what is your answer?So, all in all, what is your answer?
  • You: All in all, I think a small town gives you the best of both.All in all, I think a small town gives you the best of both.

Typical mistakes

I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
'Agree' is a verb in English, so we don't use 'am' before it.
In the city there are many peoples.
In the city there are many people.
'People' is already plural, so we don't add 's'.
The village is more quiet than the city.
The village is quieter than the city.
Short adjectives like 'quiet' usually take '-er': quieter.

Tip

Give at least one reason for your opinion and one example. Phrases like 'The main reason is...' and 'For example...' make your answer clear and strong.
