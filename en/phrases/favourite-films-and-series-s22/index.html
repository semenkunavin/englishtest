Favourite films and series in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about films and series you like and recommend them to a friend. You can use them at school, at work, or when you meet friends.

Useful phrases

  • What kind of films do you like?What kind of films do you like?
  • I'm really into sci-fi.I'm really into sci-fi.
  • My favourite series is a comedy.My favourite series is a comedy.
  • Have you seen the new season?Have you seen the new season?
  • It's about a group of friends in New York.It's about a group of friends in New York.
  • It's really funny and easy to watch.It's really funny and easy to watch.
  • The acting is great.The acting is great.
  • I didn't like the ending.I didn't like the ending.
  • It was a bit boring for me.It was a bit boring for me.
  • It's too long.It's too long.
  • I watched it last weekend.I watched it last weekend.
  • I've seen it three times.I've seen it three times.
  • You should watch it.You should watch it.
  • I really recommend it.I really recommend it.
  • It's on Netflix.It's on Netflix.
  • What's it about?What's it about?
  • Who is in it?Who is in it?
  • Is it good?Is it good?
  • Thanks for the tip!Thanks for the tip!
  • Let me know what you think.Let me know what you think.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hey, what kind of films do you like?Hey, what kind of films do you like?
  • You: I'm really into comedies and action films. What about you?I'm really into comedies and action films. What about you?
  • Partner: I love sci-fi. Have you seen the new series on Netflix?I love sci-fi. Have you seen the new series on Netflix?
  • You: No, I haven't. What's it about?No, I haven't. What's it about?
  • Partner: It's about a group of friends who travel to space. It's really exciting.It's about a group of friends who travel to space. It's really exciting.
  • You: Sounds good. Who is in it?Sounds good. Who is in it?
  • Partner: I don't know the actors, but the acting is great. You should watch it.I don't know the actors, but the acting is great. You should watch it.
  • You: OK, I'll try it. Is it long?OK, I'll try it. Is it long?
  • Partner: Eight episodes, about 40 minutes each. I really recommend it.Eight episodes, about 40 minutes each. I really recommend it.
  • You: Great, thanks for the tip! I'll watch the first episode tonight.Great, thanks for the tip! I'll watch the first episode tonight.

Typical mistakes

I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
Use 'agree' without 'am'.
I have seen it yesterday.
I saw it yesterday.
Use the past simple with a finished time like 'yesterday'.
I recommend you to watch it.
I recommend watching it. / You should watch it.
'Recommend' is usually followed by -ing or a that-clause, not 'you to'.

Tip

When you recommend a film or series, say three things: what it is (a comedy, a drama), what it is about (one sentence), and why you like it. This makes your recommendation clear and easy to answer.
