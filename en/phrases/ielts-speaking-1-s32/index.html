Work or Study in English: IELTS Speaking Part 1 Phrases

These phrases help you answer IELTS Speaking Part 1 questions about your job, your studies, or your school. Use them when the examiner asks 'Do you work or are you a student?'

Useful phrases

  • I'm a student.I am a person who studies.
  • I'm studying at university.I go to university now.
  • I'm in my second year.This is my second year of study.
  • I study business.My subject is business.
  • My major is economics.My main subject is economics.
  • I'm taking a course in English.I am doing an English course.
  • I'm preparing for exams.I am getting ready for tests.
  • I usually study in the library.I often study in the library.
  • My classes start at nine.My lessons begin at 9 o'clock.
  • I work full-time.I work all day, every week.
  • I work part-time.I work only some hours.
  • I work as a teacher.My job is teaching.
  • I work in an office.My workplace is an office.
  • I've been working here for two years.I started this job two years ago.
  • I like my job because it's interesting.I enjoy my work because it is fun to learn.
  • My job is quite busy.I have a lot to do at work.
  • I'd like to change my job.I want a different job in the future.
  • What do you study?Tell me your subject.
  • Why did you choose this subject?What made you pick this subject?
  • Do you enjoy your work?Do you like your job?

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Do you work or are you a student?Do you have a job or do you study?
  • You: I'm a student. I'm studying at university.I study at university.
  • Partner: What do you study?Tell me your subject.
  • You: I study business. My major is economics.My subject is business, and my main subject is economics.
  • Partner: Why did you choose this subject?What made you pick this subject?
  • You: I chose it because I want to work in a company. Also, the course is very practical.I picked it because I want a job in a company. The course is also very useful in real life.
  • Partner: Do you enjoy your studies?Do you like your studies?
  • You: Yes, I do. I like my classes, but sometimes I'm very busy with exams.Yes, I like my classes, but sometimes I have a lot of exam work.
  • Partner: Where do you usually study?Where do you normally study?
  • You: I usually study in the library. It's quiet there.I normally study in the library because it is quiet.
  • Partner: What would you like to do after you finish?What job do you want after your studies?
  • You: I'd like to work in an office and maybe start my own small business one day.I want to work in an office and perhaps have my own small company later.

Typical mistakes

I am student.
I am a student.
Use 'a' before a job or a role like 'student'.
I study in the university for two years.
I've been studying at university for two years.
Use 'I've been studying' for something that started in the past and continues now.
I work like a teacher.
I work as a teacher.
Use 'work as' to say your job; 'work like' means 'similar to'.

Tip

Start every Part 1 answer with a short, clear sentence, then add one more sentence with a reason or an example. Two short sentences are enough.
