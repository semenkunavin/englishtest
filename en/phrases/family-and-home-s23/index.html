Family and Home in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about your family, your home and the area where you live. They are useful when you meet a new neighbour, chat with friends, or answer simple questions about your life.

Useful phrases

  • I live with my family in a small flat.I live with my family in a small flat.
  • There are four people in my family.There are four people in my family.
  • I have one brother and one sister.I have one brother and one sister.
  • My parents live nearby.My parents live nearby.
  • We moved here two years ago.We moved here two years ago.
  • It's a quiet neighbourhood.It's a quiet neighbourhood.
  • There's a park just around the corner.There's a park just around the corner.
  • The shops are within walking distance.The shops are within walking distance.
  • How long have you lived here?How long have you lived here?
  • Do you like living in this area?Do you like living in this area?
  • What's the neighbourhood like?What's the neighbourhood like?
  • Is there a supermarket near here?Is there a supermarket near here?
  • We live on the third floor.We live on the third floor.
  • We rent a house with a small garden.We rent a house with a small garden.
  • My kids go to the local school.My kids go to the local school.
  • I get on well with my neighbours.I get on well with my neighbours.
  • The neighbours are very friendly.The neighbours are very friendly.
  • It's close to the city centre.It's close to the city centre.
  • There's a bus stop right outside.There's a bus stop right outside.
  • Welcome to the neighbourhood!Welcome to the neighbourhood!

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hi! I'm your new neighbour. I moved in yesterday.Hi! I'm your new neighbour. I moved in yesterday.
  • You: Oh, hello! Welcome to the neighbourhood. I'm Daniel.Oh, hello! Welcome to the neighbourhood. I'm Daniel.
  • Partner: Nice to meet you. How long have you lived here?Nice to meet you. How long have you lived here?
  • You: About three years. We live on the third floor with our two kids.About three years. We live on the third floor with our two kids.
  • Partner: Is it a good place for families?Is it a good place for families?
  • You: Yes, it's very quiet. There's a park just around the corner.Yes, it's very quiet. There's a park just around the corner.
  • Partner: That's good to know. Is there a supermarket near here?That's good to know. Is there a supermarket near here?
  • You: Yes, there's one on the main street, about five minutes' walk.Yes, there's one on the main street, about five minutes' walk.
  • Partner: Perfect. And what are the neighbours like?Perfect. And what are the neighbours like?
  • You: Very friendly. We get on well with most people here. I'm sure you'll like it.Very friendly. We get on well with most people here. I'm sure you'll like it.
  • Partner: Thanks! That's really helpful.Thanks! That's really helpful.
  • You: No problem. Let me know if you need anything!No problem. Let me know if you need anything!

Typical mistakes

I live in third floor.
I live on the third floor.
Use 'on' with floors of a building.
How long do you live here?
How long have you lived here?
For a situation that started in the past and continues now, use the present perfect.
There have a park near here.
There is a park near here.
'There is/are' is the correct structure to say something exists.

Tip

When you meet a new neighbour, start with a friendly 'Welcome to the neighbourhood!' and ask 'How long have you lived here?' It is simple, polite and starts a good conversation.
