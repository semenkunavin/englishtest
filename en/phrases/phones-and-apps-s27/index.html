Phones and apps in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about phones, apps and how you use the internet in everyday conversation. Use them when a friend shows you a new app or asks what you do online.

Useful phrases

  • What phone do you have?Which phone do you use?
  • I just got a new phone.I have a new phone now.
  • My battery runs out really fast.My phone loses power quickly.
  • I need to charge my phone.I have to put my phone on charge.
  • Have you tried this app?Did you test this app?
  • It's free, but there are lots of ads.It costs nothing, but it has many advertisements.
  • Can you send me the link?Please share the link with me.
  • I use it every day.I use it daily.
  • What do you use it for?How do you use it? What is its purpose?
  • It's really easy to use.It is simple to use.
  • I mostly use it for messages.I mainly use it to send messages.
  • Do you have Wi-Fi here?Is there a wireless internet connection here?
  • I'm almost out of data.I have very little mobile internet left.
  • The signal is really bad here.The phone connection is very poor here.
  • I turned off my notifications.I stopped the phone from showing alerts.
  • My phone is always full.There is no free storage on my phone.
  • I need to update the app.I must install the newest version of the app.
  • Should I download it?Is it a good idea to get it on my phone?
  • How much screen time do you have?How long do you spend on your phone each day?
  • I try not to use it too much.I try to use it less often.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hey, is that a new phone?Hello, is that a new phone?
  • You: Yes, I just got it last week. I'm still getting used to it.Yes, I got it last week. I am still learning to use it.
  • Partner: Nice. What do you use it for most?Good. What do you use it mostly for?
  • You: Mostly messages and maps. Oh, and I listen to music on the bus.Mainly for messages and maps. And I listen to music on the bus.
  • Partner: Have you tried that new language app? It's free.Did you try that new language app? It costs nothing.
  • You: Not yet. Is it easy to use?Not yet. Is it simple to use?
  • Partner: Yes, very easy. But there are lots of ads.Yes, very easy. But it has many advertisements.
  • You: That's fine. Can you send me the link?That is okay. Please send me the link.
  • Partner: Sure. Do you have Wi-Fi here? The signal is bad in this cafe.Okay. Is there Wi-Fi here? The connection is poor in this cafe.
  • You: I know. I'm almost out of data, so I'll download it at home.I know. I have very little mobile internet left, so I will download it at home.
  • Partner: Good idea. Don't forget to turn off notifications. They never stop.Good idea. Remember to turn off alerts. They never stop.
  • You: Thanks for the tip. I'll try it tonight.Thank you for the suggestion. I will try it tonight.

Typical mistakes

I have a new mobile since one week.
I've had a new phone for a week.
Use for with a length of time, and 'phone' is more natural than 'mobile'.
Can you send to me the link?
Can you send me the link?
The word order is 'send me the link', with no 'to'.
I use it for to check my email.
I use it to check my email.
After 'use it', say 'to + verb'; don't use 'for to'.

Tip

Keep three or four phone phrases ready, like 'Can you send me the link?' and 'I need to charge my phone'. They come up again and again, so you can speak faster without thinking.
