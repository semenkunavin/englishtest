A busy street in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about a busy street and what people are doing right now. Use the present continuous (am/is/are + -ing) to describe actions happening at this moment.

Useful phrases

  • What can you see in the picture?What can you see in the picture?
  • There are a lot of people on the street.There are a lot of people on the street.
  • A man is waiting at the bus stop.A man is waiting at the bus stop.
  • Two women are talking outside a shop.Two women are talking outside a shop.
  • A bus is coming down the road.A bus is coming down the road.
  • Some kids are riding their bikes.Some kids are riding their bikes.
  • A taxi is stopping near the corner.A taxi is stopping near the corner.
  • People are crossing the street.People are crossing the street.
  • What is the man in the blue jacket doing?What is the man in the blue jacket doing?
  • He is looking at his phone.He is looking at his phone.
  • Is the woman carrying a bag?Is the woman carrying a bag?
  • Yes, she is. She is carrying a big shopping bag.Yes, she is. She is carrying a big shopping bag.
  • No, he isn't. He is just standing there.No, he isn't. He is just standing there.
  • The street is very noisy and crowded.The street is very noisy and crowded.
  • A street vendor is selling fruit.A street vendor is selling fruit.
  • Someone is playing music near the café.Someone is playing music near the café.
  • How many cars are there?How many cars are there?
  • I can count about ten cars.I can count about ten cars.
  • What else is happening?What else is happening?
  • A dog is running after a ball.A dog is running after a ball.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Look at this picture of a busy street. What can you see?Look at this picture of a busy street. What can you see?
  • You: I can see a lot of people. They are walking and talking.I can see a lot of people. They are walking and talking.
  • Partner: Good. What is the man in the blue jacket doing?Good. What is the man in the blue jacket doing?
  • You: He is standing at the bus stop. He is looking at his phone.He is standing at the bus stop. He is looking at his phone.
  • Partner: Right. Is a bus coming?Right. Is a bus coming?
  • You: Yes, it is. A red bus is coming down the road.Yes, it is. A red bus is coming down the road.
  • Partner: What are the two women outside the shop doing?What are the two women outside the shop doing?
  • You: They are talking and laughing. One of them is carrying a shopping bag.They are talking and laughing. One of them is carrying a shopping bag.
  • Partner: Are the children crossing the street?Are the children crossing the street?
  • You: No, they aren't. They are riding their bikes on the pavement.No, they aren't. They are riding their bikes on the pavement.
  • Partner: Well done. Now tell me: what else is happening?Well done. Now tell me: what else is happening?

Typical mistakes

He is stand at the bus stop.
He is standing at the bus stop.
After am/is/are, use the -ing form of the verb.
They are talk outside a shop.
They are talking outside a shop.
The present continuous needs the -ing form: are + talking.
What does the man doing?
What is the man doing?
For actions happening now, use is/are + -ing, not does.

Tip

Describe the picture from left to right and use three questions: What is he/she doing? What are they doing? Is/Are ... -ing? Then answer in full sentences.
