Asking for a Day Off in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you ask your manager for a day off in a polite and clear way. You will also learn how to talk about your tasks and agree who can cover them while you are away.

Useful phrases

  • Can I ask you something?Can I ask you something?
  • Could I take a day off next week?Could I take a day off next week?
  • I'd like to take Friday off, if possible.I'd like to take Friday off, if possible.
  • Would next Thursday work for the team?Would next Thursday work for the team?
  • It's for a personal matter.It's for a personal matter.
  • I have a doctor's appointment.I have a doctor's appointment.
  • I'll make sure everything is done before I go.I'll make sure everything is done before I go.
  • I can finish my tasks before Wednesday.I can finish my tasks before Wednesday.
  • Who can cover my work while I'm away?Who can cover my work while I'm away?
  • Could you cover my emails on Friday?Could you cover my emails on Friday?
  • I can hand over the report to Sam.I can hand over the report to Sam.
  • I'll write a short handover note.I'll write a short handover note.
  • Let me check the schedule.Let me check the schedule.
  • That should be fine.That should be fine.
  • Thanks for letting me know.Thanks for letting me know.
  • Thanks for being flexible.Thanks for being flexible.
  • Is there anything urgent next week?Is there anything urgent next week?
  • I'll be back on Monday morning.I'll be back on Monday morning.
  • Please tell me if you need anything else.Please tell me if you need anything else.
  • Enjoy your day off!Enjoy your day off!

Sample dialogue

  • You: Hi, Sarah. Can I ask you something?Hi, Sarah. Can I ask you something?
  • Partner: Of course. What's up?Of course. What's up?
  • You: Could I take Friday off next week? It's for a personal matter.Could I take Friday off next week? It's for a personal matter.
  • Partner: Let me check the schedule. Is there anything urgent on Friday?Let me check the schedule. Is there anything urgent on Friday?
  • You: No, nothing urgent. I can finish the report before Wednesday.No, nothing urgent. I can finish the report before Wednesday.
  • Partner: Good. Who can cover your emails while you're away?Good. Who can cover your emails while you're away?
  • You: Sam said he can cover my emails. I'll write a short handover note for him.Sam said he can cover my emails. I'll write a short handover note for him.
  • Partner: That works for me. Friday is fine.That works for me. Friday is fine.
  • You: Thank you! I'll be back on Monday morning.Thank you! I'll be back on Monday morning.
  • Partner: No problem. Thanks for letting me know early. Enjoy your day off!No problem. Thanks for letting me know early. Enjoy your day off!

Typical mistakes

I want a day off next week.
Could I take a day off next week?
'I want' sounds too direct. Use 'Could I' to be polite.
I will not come tomorrow.
I'd like to take tomorrow off, if possible.
This is softer and more polite. You also ask instead of just telling.
Nobody do my work when I'm not here.
Who can cover my work while I'm away?
Use 'cover my work' and ask a clear question about your tasks.

Tip

Ask early, say the reason in one short sentence, and come with a plan: what you will finish and who can cover your tasks.
