Online team meeting in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you speak clearly in a weekly online team meeting. Use them to give a short status update and ask a question politely.

Useful phrases

  • Can everyone hear me?Can everyone hear me?
  • Sorry, I was on mute.Sorry, I was on mute.
  • Let's get started.Let's get started.
  • Can you see my screen?Can you see my screen?
  • I'll share my screen.I'll share my screen.
  • Just a quick update from me.Just a quick update from me.
  • I'm working on the report.I'm working on the report.
  • I finished the first draft.I finished the first draft.
  • I'm waiting for feedback from the client.I'm waiting for feedback from the client.
  • I'll send it by Friday.I'll send it by Friday.
  • Everything is on track.Everything is on track.
  • I have a question about the deadline.I have a question about the deadline.
  • Could you explain that again, please?Could you explain that again, please?
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that.Sorry, I didn't catch that.
  • Can you hear me now?Can you hear me now?
  • I think we should ask the client first.I think we should ask the client first.
  • That sounds good to me.That sounds good to me.
  • I agree with Anna.I agree with Anna.
  • Thanks, everyone. Let's move on.Thanks, everyone. Let's move on.
  • I'll follow up after the meeting.I'll follow up after the meeting.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hi everyone, can you hear me?Hi everyone, can you hear me?
  • You: Yes, I can hear you clearly.Yes, I can hear you clearly.
  • Partner: Great. Let's start with updates. B, can you go first?Great. Let's start with updates. B, can you go first?
  • You: Sure. Just a quick update from me. I'm working on the report and I finished the first draft.Sure. Just a quick update from me. I'm working on the report and I finished the first draft.
  • Partner: Good. When can you send it?Good. When can you send it?
  • You: I'll send it by Friday. I'm waiting for feedback from the client.I'll send it by Friday. I'm waiting for feedback from the client.
  • Partner: OK. Any questions?OK. Any questions?
  • You: Yes, I have a question about the deadline. Is it still next Monday?Yes, I have a question about the deadline. Is it still next Monday?
  • Partner: Yes, it is. Thanks for asking.Yes, it is. Thanks for asking.
  • You: Great. That sounds good to me.Great. That sounds good to me.
  • Partner: Thanks, everyone. Let's move on to the next topic.Thanks, everyone. Let's move on to the next topic.

Typical mistakes

I am working on report since Monday.
I have been working on the report since Monday.
Use the present perfect continuous for actions that started in the past and continue now.
I have a question to the deadline.
I have a question about the deadline.
The correct preposition is 'about' when you mean 'related to'.
I will send it until Friday.
I will send it by Friday.
Use 'by' for a deadline, not 'until'.

Tip

Before the meeting, write down 2–3 short sentences for your update. Say them out loud once. This helps you speak clearly and calmly.
