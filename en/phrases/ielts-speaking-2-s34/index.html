IELTS Speaking Part 2: A Place to Visit in English

These phrases help you talk about a place you would like to visit in IELTS Speaking Part 2. Use them to describe the place, explain why you want to go, and answer follow-up questions.

Useful phrases

  • I would like to visit...I want to go to...
  • It's a popular tourist destination.Many people go there on holiday.
  • It's famous for its...It is well-known for...
  • The weather is usually...The weather is often...
  • I have always wanted to go there.I have wanted to visit for a long time.
  • It's a great place to relax.It is a good place to rest and feel calm.
  • There are many things to do.You can do lots of activities.
  • The food is delicious.The food tastes very good.
  • I would love to try...I really want to try...
  • It's a must-see place.It is a place you should definitely visit.
  • I plan to go there next year.I intend to visit next year.
  • The people are friendly.The local people are nice.
  • It's a bit expensive, but worth it.It costs a lot, but it is good value.
  • I prefer places with...I like places that have...
  • It's not too crowded.There are not too many people.
  • I would recommend it to others.I would suggest it to other people.
  • The best time to go is...The ideal time to visit is...
  • I'm interested in...I want to know more about...
  • It's a unique experience.It is a special experience.
  • I hope I can go there someday.I wish to visit one day.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Describe a place you would like to visit.Talk about a place you want to go to.
  • You: I would like to visit Japan, especially Tokyo.I want to go to Japan, particularly Tokyo.
  • Partner: Why do you want to go there?What is the reason you want to go there?
  • You: It's famous for its culture and food. I love sushi and I have always wanted to try real Japanese sushi.It is well-known for its culture and food. I love sushi and I have always wanted to eat authentic Japanese sushi.
  • Partner: What would you do there?What activities would you do there?
  • You: I would visit temples, go shopping, and try local food. There are many things to do.I would go to temples, shop, and eat local food. There are lots of activities.
  • Partner: Who would you go with?Who would you travel with?
  • You: I would go with my best friend because we both love traveling.My best friend and I would go because we both enjoy traveling.
  • Partner: When would you like to go?What time would you like to go?
  • You: I plan to go next spring. The weather is usually nice then.I intend to go next spring. The weather is often good at that time.
  • Partner: Is it expensive?Does it cost a lot?
  • You: It's a bit expensive, but worth it. I hope I can go there someday.It costs quite a lot, but it is good value. I wish to visit one day.

Typical mistakes

I want go to Japan.
I want to go to Japan.
Use 'to' after 'want' before a verb.
It's famous of its food.
It's famous for its food.
The correct preposition is 'for'.
I have ever wanted to go there.
I have always wanted to go there.
Use 'always' with 'have wanted' to mean 'for a long time'.

Tip

When describing a place, add details: say what you can do there, what the food is like, and how you feel about it. Use adjectives like 'delicious', 'friendly', and 'relaxing'.
