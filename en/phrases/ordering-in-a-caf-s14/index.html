Ordering in a café in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you order food and drinks in a café, ask what is in the food, and pay at the end. Use them when you travel or visit a café where people speak English.

  • I'd like a coffee, please.I'd like a coffee, please.

  • Partner: Good morning! How can I help you?Good morning! How can I help you?

Typical mistakes

I want a coffee.
I'd like a coffee, please.
'I want' sounds rude in a café. 'I'd like ... please' is polite and natural.
Give me the bill.
Could I have the bill, please?
Use 'Could I have ...' to ask politely. 'Give me' sounds like an order.
How much cost it?
How much is it?
The question needs the verb 'is'. 'How much cost it?' is not correct English.

Tip

Say 'please' when you order and 'thank you' when you get your food or drink. In the UK and US, it is also common to leave a small tip (about 10–15%) in a café.
