Food from Your Country in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about food you love and explain how to cook a dish from your country. Use them in conversations with friends or when someone asks about your culture.

Useful phrases

  • What's your favourite food?What's your favourite food?
  • I really love...I really love...
  • My favourite dish is...My favourite dish is...
  • It's a traditional dish from my country.It's a traditional dish from my country.
  • How do you make it?How do you make it?
  • Can you tell me the recipe?Can you tell me the recipe?
  • First, you need...First, you need...
  • Then, you...Then, you...
  • After that, you...After that, you...
  • Finally, you...Finally, you...
  • It's easy to make.It's easy to make.
  • It takes about... minutes.It takes about... minutes.
  • You cook it for... minutes.You cook it for... minutes.
  • It's delicious!It's delicious!
  • It's spicy/sweet/salty.It's spicy/sweet/salty.
  • Would you like to try some?Would you like to try some?
  • I'd love to!I'd love to!
  • What ingredients do you need?What ingredients do you need?
  • You need some... and a little...You need some... and a little...
  • Serve it hot/cold.Serve it hot/cold.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hey! What's your favourite food?Hey! What's your favourite food?
  • You: My favourite dish is paella. It's a traditional dish from Spain.My favourite dish is paella. It's a traditional dish from Spain.
  • Partner: That sounds great! How do you make it?That sounds great! How do you make it?
  • You: First, you need rice, chicken, seafood, and vegetables.First, you need rice, chicken, seafood, and vegetables.
  • Partner: What do you do next?What do you do next?
  • You: Then, you cook the chicken and vegetables in a big pan.Then, you cook the chicken and vegetables in a big pan.
  • Partner: And after that?And after that?
  • You: After that, you add the rice and water. You cook it for about 20 minutes.After that, you add the rice and water. You cook it for about 20 minutes.
  • Partner: Is it easy to make?Is it easy to make?
  • You: Yes, it's easy! Finally, you serve it hot with lemon.Yes, it's easy! Finally, you serve it hot with lemon.
  • Partner: It sounds delicious! Would you like to try some?It sounds delicious! Would you like to try some?
  • You: I'd love to! Thanks!I'd love to! Thanks!

Typical mistakes

I like very much this food.
I like this food very much.
In English, 'very much' usually comes at the end of the sentence.
First you need rice, after you cook it.
First you need rice, then you cook it.
Use 'then' to show the next step, not 'after' alone.
It's a food traditional from my country.
It's a traditional food from my country.
Adjectives come before nouns in English.

Tip

When you explain a recipe, use sequence words like 'first', 'then', 'after that', and 'finally' to make your steps clear.
