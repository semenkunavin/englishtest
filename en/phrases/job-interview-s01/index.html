Job interview: tell me about yourself in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you in a first job interview at an international company. You can use them to introduce yourself and talk about your experience and strengths.

Useful phrases

  • Good morning, thank you for seeing me today.Good morning, thank you for seeing me today.
  • My name is Ana and I'm from Spain.My name is Ana and I'm from Spain.
  • Can I tell you a little about myself?Can I tell you a little about myself?
  • I'm a recent graduate in business.I'm a recent graduate in business.
  • I studied marketing at university.I studied marketing at university.
  • I worked for six months in a small shop.I worked for six months in a small shop.
  • I'm good with people and I learn fast.I'm good with people and I learn fast.
  • My main strength is that I'm well organized.My main strength is that I'm well organized.
  • I'm interested in this job because I love working with customers.I'm interested in this job because I love working with customers.
  • I'm a team player and I'm not afraid to ask questions.I'm a team player and I'm not afraid to ask questions.
  • Could you tell me more about yourself?Could you tell me more about yourself?
  • What did you study?What did you study?
  • Do you have any work experience?Do you have any work experience?
  • What are your strengths?What are your strengths?
  • Why do you want to work here?Why do you want to work here?
  • How would your friends describe you?How would your friends describe you?
  • That sounds interesting. Can you give me an example?That sounds interesting. Can you give me an example?
  • What do you do in your free time?What do you do in your free time?
  • Thank you for your time today.Thank you for your time today.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Good morning! Please sit down. Could you tell me a little about yourself?Good morning! Please sit down. Could you tell me a little about yourself?
  • You: Good morning, and thank you for seeing me today. My name is Ana and I'm from Spain.Good morning, and thank you for seeing me today. My name is Ana and I'm from Spain.
  • Partner: Nice to meet you, Ana. What did you study?Nice to meet you, Ana. What did you study?
  • You: I studied marketing at university. I finished last year.I studied marketing at university. I finished last year.
  • Partner: Do you have any work experience?Do you have any work experience?
  • You: Yes. I worked for six months in a small shop. I talked to customers every day.Yes. I worked for six months in a small shop. I talked to customers every day.
  • Partner: Good. And what are your strengths?Good. And what are your strengths?
  • You: I'm well organized and I learn fast. I'm also good with people.I'm well organized and I learn fast. I'm also good with people.
  • Partner: That sounds great. Why do you want to work here?That sounds great. Why do you want to work here?
  • You: I'm interested in this job because I love working with customers. And I want to learn in an international team.I'm interested in this job because I love working with customers. And I want to learn in an international team.
  • Partner: Thank you, Ana. Do you have any questions for us?Thank you, Ana. Do you have any questions for us?
  • You: Yes, one question: what does a normal day look like in this job?Yes, one question: what does a normal day look like in this job?

Typical mistakes

I am working in a shop for six months.
I worked in a shop for six months.
For a finished experience in the past, use the past simple.
I have 22 years.
I'm 22 years old.
In English, we say 'I'm ... years old', not 'I have ... years'.
I am very good in computers.
I'm very good with computers.
We say 'good at' a skill or 'good with' people or things.

Tip

Prepare a short answer of about 30 seconds about yourself: your name, what you studied, one work experience, and one strength. Say it out loud a few times before the interview.
