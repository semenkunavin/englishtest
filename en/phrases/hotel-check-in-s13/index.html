Hotel check-in in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you check in at a hotel and fix a problem with your booking. They are useful when the reservation is wrong, missing or different from what you expected.

Useful phrases

  • Good evening. I have a reservation under the name Silva.Good evening. I have a reservation under the name Silva.
  • Could you check it again, please?Could you check it again, please?
  • I booked a double room for two nights.I booked a double room for two nights.
  • I'm sorry, but there seems to be a problem with my booking.I'm sorry, but there seems to be a problem with my booking.
  • Here is my booking confirmation.Here is my booking confirmation.
  • I have the confirmation email on my phone.I have the confirmation email on my phone.
  • Is there another room available tonight?Is there another room available tonight?
  • Would it be possible to change my room?Would it be possible to change my room?
  • I booked a room with a sea view.I booked a room with a sea view.
  • Could I speak to the manager, please?Could I speak to the manager, please?
  • I've already paid for the room.I've already paid for the room.
  • Can you help me find a solution?Can you help me find a solution?
  • What time is breakfast served?What time is breakfast served?
  • Could I have the Wi-Fi password, please?Could I have the Wi-Fi password, please?
  • Let me check the details for you.Let me check the details for you.
  • I'm very sorry about that. I'll see what I can do.I'm very sorry about that. I'll see what I can do.
  • There is a room available, but it is a single room.There is a room available, but it is a single room.
  • Would you like to wait a few minutes?Would you like to wait a few minutes?
  • I can offer you a free upgrade.I can offer you a free upgrade.
  • Is there anything else I can help you with?Is there anything else I can help you with?

Sample dialogue

  • You: Good evening. I have a reservation under the name Silva.Good evening. I have a reservation under the name Silva.
  • Partner: Good evening. Let me check the details for you. I'm sorry, but I can't find your name.Good evening. Let me check the details for you. I'm sorry, but I can't find your name.
  • You: I'm sorry, but there seems to be a problem with my booking. I have the confirmation email on my phone.I'm sorry, but there seems to be a problem with my booking. I have the confirmation email on my phone.
  • Partner: Thank you. I see it now. You booked a double room with a sea view for two nights.Thank you. I see it now. You booked a double room with a sea view for two nights.
  • You: Yes, that's right. But I asked for a room with a balcony.Yes, that's right. But I asked for a room with a balcony.
  • Partner: I'm very sorry about that. The balcony rooms are all full tonight. I can offer you a free upgrade to a larger room.I'm very sorry about that. The balcony rooms are all full tonight. I can offer you a free upgrade to a larger room.
  • You: A larger room sounds good. Does it have a sea view?A larger room sounds good. Does it have a sea view?
  • Partner: Yes, it has a great sea view. Here is your key. Your room is on the third floor.Yes, it has a great sea view. Here is your key. Your room is on the third floor.
  • You: Thank you. What time is breakfast served?Thank you. What time is breakfast served?
  • Partner: Breakfast is from seven to ten. Is there anything else I can help you with?Breakfast is from seven to ten. Is there anything else I can help you with?
  • You: Could I have the Wi-Fi password, please?Could I have the Wi-Fi password, please?
  • Partner: Of course. It's on your key card. Enjoy your stay!Of course. It's on your key card. Enjoy your stay!

Typical mistakes

I have a reservation for name Silva.
I have a reservation under the name Silva.
In English, we say 'under the name' when we give the name for a booking.
I book a double room.
I booked a double room.
The booking happened in the past, so use the past simple 'booked'.
Can you make a solution?
Can you help me find a solution?
We say 'find a solution' or 'solve a problem', not 'make a solution'.

Tip

Stay calm and polite, and always show your booking confirmation. If the hotel can't give you the room you booked, ask politely what they can offer you instead.
