Daily Routine and Health in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you talk about your day, your sleep and your sport habits in simple, natural English. Use them with friends, in class or when you talk to a coach about your routine.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Hi! Tell me about your daily routine.Hi! Tell me about your daily routine.
  • You: I usually get up at seven and go to work by bus.I usually get up at seven and go to work by bus.
  • Partner: How many hours do you sleep?How many hours do you sleep?
  • You: About six. I go to bed late and I'm often tired.About six. I go to bed late and I'm often tired.
  • Partner: Do you do any sport?Do you do any sport?
  • You: Not much. It's hard to find time for sport.Not much. It's hard to find time for sport.
  • Partner: Maybe you could try short walks after work.Maybe you could try short walks after work.
  • You: That sounds good. I want to be more active.That sounds good. I want to be more active.
  • Partner: And try to go to bed before eleven.And try to go to bed before eleven.

Typical mistakes

I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
'Agree' is a verb, so we don't use 'am' before it.
I go to bed at the night.
I go to bed at night.
We say 'at night', not 'at the night'.
How much hours do you sleep?
How many hours do you sleep?
Use 'how many' with countable nouns like 'hours'.

Tip

Keep your answers short and add one extra detail, like 'I go to bed at eleven, but I'm still tired.' This makes your answer sound natural and gives the other person something to reply to.
