Small Talk with Your Manager in English: Phrases and a Sample Dialogue

These phrases help you make small talk with your manager in casual situations, like before a meeting or in the office kitchen. Use them to sound natural and friendly in English.

Useful phrases

  • How was your weekend?How was your weekend?
  • Did you do anything fun?Did you do anything fun?
  • The weather is nice today, isn't it?The weather is nice today, isn't it?
  • It looks like it's going to rain.It looks like it's going to rain.
  • I can't believe it's already Friday.I can't believe it's already Friday.
  • Any plans for the weekend?Any plans for the weekend?
  • I'm looking forward to the weekend.I'm looking forward to the weekend.
  • How's the project going?How's the project going?
  • Busy day today?Busy day today?
  • I heard you had a meeting with the client.I heard you had a meeting with the client.
  • Good luck with your presentation.Good luck with your presentation.
  • Let me know if you need anything.Let me know if you need anything.
  • I like your jacket.I like your jacket.
  • That sounds great.That sounds great.
  • I agree.I agree.
  • Really? Tell me more.Really? Tell me more.
  • Oh, interesting.Oh, interesting.
  • I see.I see.
  • Well, I should get back to work.Well, I should get back to work.
  • It was nice talking to you.It was nice talking to you.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Morning! How was your weekend?Morning! How was your weekend?
  • You: It was great, thanks. I went hiking with some friends.It was great, thanks. I went hiking with some friends.
  • Partner: That sounds fun. The weather was perfect for it.That sounds fun. The weather was perfect for it.
  • You: Yes, it was sunny but not too hot. How about you?Yes, it was sunny but not too hot. How about you?
  • Partner: I just relaxed at home. Did you see the game on Sunday?I just relaxed at home. Did you see the game on Sunday?
  • You: No, I missed it. Was it good?No, I missed it. Was it good?
  • Partner: It was amazing! Our team won in the last minute.It was amazing! Our team won in the last minute.
  • You: Wow, I wish I had seen it.Wow, I wish I had seen it.
  • Partner: Anyway, we have a meeting in a few minutes. Are you ready?Anyway, we have a meeting in a few minutes. Are you ready?
  • You: Yes, I am. I'll just grab a coffee and head over.Yes, I am. I'll just grab a coffee and head over.
  • Partner: Great. See you there.Great. See you there.
  • You: See you.See you.

Typical mistakes

How was your weekend? I am fine.
How was your weekend? It was good, thanks.
When someone asks about your weekend, answer about the weekend, not about yourself.
The weather is nice today, no?
The weather is nice today, isn't it?
In English, use a tag question like 'isn't it?' to check agreement, not 'no?'.
I have a meeting with the client. I am very busy.
I have a meeting with the client, so I'm quite busy.
Use 'so' to connect the two ideas naturally; 'quite' sounds more natural than 'very' here.

Tip

Keep small talk short and positive. Ask simple questions and listen actively. It's okay to talk about light topics like weather, weekend, or sports.
