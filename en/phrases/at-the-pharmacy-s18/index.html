At the Pharmacy in English: phrases and a sample dialogue

These phrases help you when you feel ill and need medicine from a pharmacy. You can explain your symptoms, ask for what you need, and understand the pharmacist's questions.

Useful phrases

  • Good morning. I'd like some help, please.Good morning. I'd like some help, please.
  • I have a bad headache.I have a bad headache.
  • I've had a cough for three days.I've had a cough for three days.
  • I have a sore throat and a runny nose.I have a sore throat and a runny nose.
  • I think I have a cold.I think I have a cold.
  • I feel sick and I have a stomach ache.I feel sick and I have a stomach ache.
  • I have a temperature.I have a temperature.
  • It hurts when I move my arm.It hurts when I move my arm.
  • Do you have anything for a cough?Do you have anything for a cough?
  • Can you recommend something for a headache?Can you recommend something for a headache?
  • I'd like some painkillers, please.I'd like some painkillers, please.
  • Do I need a prescription for this?Do I need a prescription for this?
  • How many times a day should I take it?How many times a day should I take it?
  • Should I take it with food?Should I take it with food?
  • Are there any side effects?Are there any side effects?
  • How much does it cost?How much does it cost?
  • How long have you had these symptoms?How long have you had these symptoms?
  • Are you allergic to anything?Are you allergic to anything?
  • Take one tablet twice a day after meals.Take one tablet twice a day after meals.
  • If it doesn't get better in a few days, see a doctor.If it doesn't get better in a few days, see a doctor.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Good morning. How can I help you?Good morning. How can I help you?
  • You: Hi. I have a bad headache and a sore throat.Hi. I have a bad headache and a sore throat.
  • Partner: I see. How long have you had these symptoms?I see. How long have you had these symptoms?
  • You: Since yesterday evening. I think I have a cold.Since yesterday evening. I think I have a cold.
  • Partner: Do you have a temperature?Do you have a temperature?
  • You: Yes, a little. Can you recommend something?Yes, a little. Can you recommend something?
  • Partner: This is good for headaches and sore throats. Take one tablet twice a day.This is good for headaches and sore throats. Take one tablet twice a day.
  • You: Should I take it with food?Should I take it with food?
  • Partner: Yes, after meals. And drink lots of water.Yes, after meals. And drink lots of water.
  • You: Great. How much does it cost?Great. How much does it cost?
  • Partner: It's six pounds. If it doesn't get better in a few days, see a doctor.It's six pounds. If it doesn't get better in a few days, see a doctor.
  • You: Thank you very much. Goodbye.Thank you very much. Goodbye.

Typical mistakes

I have headache since three days.
I've had a headache for three days.
Use 'a' before 'headache', 'for' with a length of time, and the present perfect for something that started in the past.
Give me medicine for cough.
Could I have something for a cough, please?
'Could I have...' is more polite than 'Give me', and we say 'a cough' with 'a'.
How I take it?
How should I take it?
Questions need 'should' here, and the word order is 'How should I...'.

Tip

Before you go to the pharmacy, write down your main symptoms and how long you've had them. It makes the conversation much easier.
