Agree or Disagree About Education in English: Phrases and a Sample Dialogue

These phrases help you say if you agree or disagree with ideas about education and explain your opinion. Use them in class discussions, speaking exams and everyday conversations.

Useful phrases

  • I agree with this idea.I agree with this idea.
  • I completely agree.I completely agree.
  • That's a good point.That's a good point.
  • You're right about that.You're right about that.
  • I think so too.I think so too.
  • I agree, because education is important.I agree, because education is important.
  • That's true, in my opinion.That's true, in my opinion.
  • I partly agree with you.I partly agree with you.
  • I see your point, but I disagree.I see your point, but I disagree.
  • I'm not sure about that.I'm not sure about that.
  • I don't really agree.I don't really agree.
  • I disagree with this statement.I disagree with this statement.
  • I don't think that's true.I don't think that's true.
  • In my opinion, exams are not always useful.In my opinion, exams are not always useful.
  • For example, many students learn better by doing.For example, many students learn better by doing.
  • That's because some people learn in different ways.That's because some people learn in different ways.
  • On the other hand, teachers are very important.On the other hand, teachers are very important.
  • Can you explain why you think that?Can you explain why you think that?
  • What do you mean by that?What do you mean by that?
  • To sum up, I agree with this idea.To sum up, I agree with this idea.

Sample dialogue

  • Partner: Here is a statement: school exams are the best way to check what students know. Do you agree?Here is a statement: school exams are the best way to check what students know. Do you agree?
  • You: I partly agree, but I don't think exams show everything.I partly agree, but I don't think exams show everything.
  • Partner: Can you explain why you think that?Can you explain why you think that?
  • You: Yes. Some students are nervous in exams, so they get low marks even when they know a lot.Yes. Some students are nervous in exams, so they get low marks even when they know a lot.
  • Partner: That's a good point. What other ways can teachers use?That's a good point. What other ways can teachers use?
  • You: For example, projects and group work. In my opinion, they show real skills.For example, projects and group work. In my opinion, they show real skills.
  • Partner: But exams are fair for everyone, aren't they?But exams are fair for everyone, aren't they?
  • You: That's true, in a way. On the other hand, not everyone is good at tests.That's true, in a way. On the other hand, not everyone is good at tests.
  • Partner: So what is your final answer?So what is your final answer?
  • You: To sum up, I agree that exams are useful, but teachers should also use other ways.To sum up, I agree that exams are useful, but teachers should also use other ways.

Typical mistakes

I am agree with you.
I agree with you.
'Agree' is a verb, so do not use 'am' before it.
I don't agree about this idea.
I don't agree with this idea.
We say 'agree with' an idea or a person.
In my opinion, I think education is important.
In my opinion, education is important.
Use only one opinion phrase, not two together.

Tip

Always give a reason after you agree or disagree: use 'because', 'for example' or 'that's because'. This makes your answer stronger and longer in a speaking exam.
