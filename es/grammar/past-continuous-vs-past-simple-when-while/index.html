Past continuous vs past simple: when y while (A2)

Usamos el past continuous para describir una acción que estaba en progreso en el pasado, y el past simple para la acción corta que la interrumpió. Con when y while unimos las dos ideas: una acción de fondo y un evento. Es muy útil para contar anécdotas de trabajo y de viajes.

  1. El past continuous se forma con was/were + verbo en -ing: I was working, they were traveling.
  2. El past simple de los verbos regulares termina en -ed (called, arrived); muchos irregulares cambian (saw, left).
  3. Usa while + past continuous para la acción larga: While I was waiting, my boss called me.
  4. Usa when + past simple para el evento corto: I was having lunch when the meeting started.
  5. Con when también puedes usar past continuous: When I was living in Quito, I met Carla.
  6. Ordena las dos partes con coma: While we were driving, it started to rain. o It started to rain while we were driving.

Ejemplos

  • While I was preparing the report, my coworker called me.Mientras yo preparaba el informe, mi compañero de trabajo me llamó.
  • Diego was traveling to Medellín when he lost his passport.Diego viajaba a Medellín cuando perdió su pasaporte.
  • We were having a meeting when the internet stopped working.Estábamos en una reunión cuando el internet dejó de funcionar.
  • Valentina was waiting for her flight while she answered emails.Valentina esperaba su vuelo mientras respondía correos.
  • I was walking to the office when I saw Lucía.Yo caminaba a la oficina cuando vi a Lucía.
  • While they were visiting Cusco, they took many photos.Mientras visitaban Cusco, tomaron muchas fotos.

Errores típicos

While I worked, the phone rang.
While I was working, the phone rang.
Después de while usamos past continuous para la acción en progreso.
I was cook dinner when Ana arrived.
I was cooking dinner when Ana arrived.
El past continuous necesita el verbo en -ing: was cooking.
When she was calling, I answered immediately.
When she called, I answered immediately.
El evento corto y puntual va en past simple: called.

Ponte a prueba

  1. While Mateo ___ in Bogotá, he learned a lot about sales.

  2. I was checking my emails when my boss ___.

  3. They ___ to Lima when the bus broke down.

  4. ___ Sofía was having lunch, she got a message from her client.

  5. We were waiting at the airport when we ___ the announcement.
