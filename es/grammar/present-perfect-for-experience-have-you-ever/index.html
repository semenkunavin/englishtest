Present perfect para experiencias: Have you ever...? (A2)

El present perfect con have you ever...? sirve para preguntar si alguien ha tenido una experiencia en algún momento de su vida, sin decir cuándo. Se usa mucho para hablar de trabajos, viajes y cosas que has hecho o nunca has hecho.

  1. Para preguntar por experiencias usa Have you ever + past participle?: Have you ever worked abroad?
  2. Para responder sí, usa Yes, I have; para responder no, usa No, I haven't (o never): No, I've never traveled by plane.
  3. El verbo va en past participle: been, worked, visited, eaten. Con verbos regulares agrega -ed; los irregulares tienen forma propia.
  4. Con ever en preguntas y never en negativas: Have you ever met a famous person? / I have never met one.
  5. Usa been to para lugares visitados: I have been to Guatemala (he ido y vuelto). No uses gone to para experiencias.
  6. No menciones el momento exacto con present perfect: di I have visited Lima, no I have visited Lima last year.

Ejemplos

  • Have you ever worked in another country?¿Alguna vez has trabajado en otro país?
  • Yes, I have. I worked in Costa Rica for two years.Sí. Trabajé en Costa Rica durante dos años.
  • Marta has never traveled by plane, but she wants to visit Mexico.Marta nunca ha viajado en avión, pero quiere visitar México.
  • I have been to Bogotá three times for work.He ido a Bogotá tres veces por trabajo.
  • Have you ever eaten Peruvian food?¿Alguna vez has comido comida peruana?
  • Diego hasn't found a job in his city yet, so he is looking online.Diego todavía no ha encontrado trabajo en su ciudad, así que está buscando en línea.

Errores típicos

Have you ever went to Chile?
Have you ever been to Chile?
Después de have se usa el past participle (been), no el past simple (went).
I have visited Cartagena last year.
I visited Cartagena last year.
Con un tiempo pasado específico (last year) se usa el past simple, no el present perfect.
She have worked in Panama.
She has worked in Panama.
Con he, she, it el auxiliar es has, no have.

Ponte a prueba

  1. ___ you ever worked with people from other countries?

  2. Ana has ___ been to Brazil, but she wants to go.

  3. I have ___ in three different companies.

  4. Have you ever ___ Salvadoran food?

  5. Pablo ___ traveled by plane. He is a little nervous.
