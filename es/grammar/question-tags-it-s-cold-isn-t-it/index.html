Question tags en inglés: It's cold, isn't it? (nivel B1)

Las question tags son esas pequeñas preguntas que se añaden al final de una frase, como isn't it? o aren't you?. Se usan en el inglés hablado para confirmar información, buscar acuerdo o simplemente mantener una conversación. Son muy comunes en el trabajo y en los viajes, por ejemplo al hacer small talk con un colega o al registrarte en un hotel.

  1. Si la frase principal es afirmativa, la question tag es negativa: You're from Costa Rica, aren't you?
  2. Si la frase principal es negativa, la question tag es afirmativa: You didn't book the flight, did you?
  3. La question tag repite el auxiliar de la frase: be, do/does/did, have, can, will, etc. She works here, doesn't she?
  4. Si no hay auxiliar, usa do, does o did según el tiempo: He called the client, didn't he?
  5. El pronombre de la tag siempre va en forma de sujeto: María is your manager, isn't she? (no isn't María?).
  6. I am forma la tag aren't I?: I'm late for the meeting, aren't I?

Ejemplos

  • It's cold in Bogotá today, isn't it?Hace frío en Bogotá hoy, ¿verdad?
  • You're coming to the workshop in Lima, aren't you?Vienes al taller en Lima, ¿verdad?
  • Diego hasn't sent the report yet, has he?Diego todavía no ha enviado el informe, ¿verdad?
  • Your flight leaves at 6 a.m., doesn't it?Tu vuelo sale a las 6 de la mañana, ¿verdad?
  • We can pay with a card at the hotel, can't we?Podemos pagar con tarjeta en el hotel, ¿verdad?
  • Valeria didn't call the client this morning, did she?Valeria no llamó al cliente esta mañana, ¿verdad?

Errores típicos

You are from Quito, aren't it?
You are from Quito, aren't you?
La tag debe repetir el sujeto de la frase: you, no it.
She works here, doesn't it?
She works here, doesn't she?
Con she usamos el pronombre she en la tag, no it.
He didn't finish the project, didn't he?
He didn't finish the project, did he?
Si la frase es negativa, la tag debe ser afirmativa.

Ponte a prueba

  1. It's hot in Cartagena, ___?

  2. Ana works in your office, ___?

  3. You didn't book the hotel, ___?

  4. We can take the early bus, ___?

  5. I'm on time for the meeting, ___?
