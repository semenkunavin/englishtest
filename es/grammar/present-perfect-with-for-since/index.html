Present perfect con for y since: guía A2

El present perfect con for y since sirve para decir cuánto tiempo lleva algo: un trabajo, un viaje, una amistad. Usas for con una cantidad de tiempo (tres años, dos meses) y since con el momento en que empezó (2020, enero, el lunes).

  1. Usa have/has + participio pasado antes de for o since: I have worked here for five years.
  2. for va con un período de tiempo: for two weeks, for a long time, for six months.
  3. since va con un punto en el tiempo: since 2019, since March, since I was a child.
  4. Con he/she/it usa has: She has lived in Lima since 2021.
  5. No uses el presente simple para indicar duración: di I have had this job for a year, no I have this job for a year.
  6. Puedes responder con for + período: How long have you been in Mexico? — For three months.

Ejemplos

  • I have worked at this company in Bogotá for six years.He trabajado en esta empresa en Bogotá durante seis años.
  • She has studied English since 2022.Ella ha estudiado inglés desde 2022.
  • We have traveled around Peru for three weeks.Hemos viajado por Perú durante tres semanas.
  • Juan has had his job at the airline since January.Juan tiene su trabajo en la aerolínea desde enero.
  • They have lived in Santiago for a long time.Ellos han vivido en Santiago por mucho tiempo.
  • I have known Lucía since we were in school.Conozco a Lucía desde que estábamos en la escuela.

Errores típicos

I work here since 2021.
I have worked here since 2021.
Para hablar de duración hasta ahora necesitas el present perfect, no el presente simple.
She has lived in Quito for 2019.
She has lived in Quito since 2019.
2019 es un punto en el tiempo, así que va con since, no con for.
We have traveled for two months ago.
We have traveled for two months.
ago indica pasado terminado; con for solo pones el período de tiempo.

Ponte a prueba

  1. I have worked at the hotel in Cartagena ___ three years.

  2. María has lived in Buenos Aires ___ 2020.

  3. They have known each other ___ they started university.

  4. We have been on this trip ___ ten days.

  5. Diego ___ worked at the airport since March.
