Past continuous en inglés: I was watching TV (nivel A2)

El past continuous es el pasado continuo en inglés: habla de una acción que estaba en progreso en un momento del pasado. Se usa para describir qué estabas haciendo cuando ocurrió otra cosa, por ejemplo en el trabajo o en un viaje.

  1. Se forma con was/were + verbo en -ing: I was watching TV, they were working.
  2. Was va con I, he, she, it; were va con you, we, they.
  3. Para negar, agrega not: I was not (wasn't) watching TV; we weren't working.
  4. Para preguntar, invierte el orden: Were you watching TV? / What was she doing?
  5. Se usa para una acción en progreso: At 8 p.m. I was studying.
  6. Se combina con el past simple para una acción que interrumpe: I was working when my boss called.

Ejemplos

  • Last night I was watching TV when Sofía called me.Anoche estaba viendo TV cuando Sofía me llamó.
  • They were working in Bogotá last week.Ellos estaban trabajando en Bogotá la semana pasada.
  • Was Diego sleeping at 11 p.m.?¿Estaba Diego durmiendo a las 11 de la noche?
  • We weren't traveling; we were at the office.No estábamos viajando; estábamos en la oficina.
  • What were you doing when the boss arrived?¿Qué estabas haciendo cuando llegó el jefe?
  • Camila was reading a report while her team was having lunch.Camila estaba leyendo un informe mientras su equipo almorzaba.

Errores típicos

I was watch TV.
I was watching TV.
Después de was/were el verbo va en -ing, no en forma base.
They was working in Lima.
They were working in Lima.
Con they se usa were, no was.
Were you watched TV?
Were you watching TV?
En el past continuous usamos watching (-ing), no el past participle.

Ponte a prueba

  1. At 9 a.m. I ___ answering emails in the office.

  2. Ana and Luis ___ traveling to Quito last week.

  3. She wasn't ___ the report yesterday.

  4. ___ you watching the meeting when I called?

  5. We were having lunch when my phone ___.
