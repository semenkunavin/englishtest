Present perfect continuous (I've been waiting): guía B1

El present perfect continuous conecta una acción con el presente: algo empezó antes y sigue ocurriendo o acaba de terminar. Se usa mucho para hablar de trabajo, viajes y esperas, sobre todo cuando te interesa la duración o el proceso. Su forma es have/has been + verbo en -ing.

  1. Forma afirmativa: I / you / we / they have been + -ing y he / she / it has been + -ing. Ejemplo: She has been working.
  2. Negativa: haven't / hasn't been + -ing. Ejemplo: We haven't been waiting long.
  3. Pregunta: Have/Has + sujeto + been + -ing. Ejemplo: Have you been traveling? Respuesta corta: Yes, I have / No, I haven't.
  4. Con for + periodo (for two hours, for three days) y since + punto de inicio (since Monday, since 8 a.m.).
  5. Sirve para acciones que continúan hasta ahora (I've been waiting for the bus) y para acciones recientes con resultado visible (Your eyes are red. Have you been crying?).
  6. Si dices cuántas veces o cuántas cosas completas, usa present perfect simple: I've sent three emails, no I've been sending three emails.

Ejemplos

  • Lucía has been working at the bank in Medellín for six months.Lucía lleva seis meses trabajando en el banco en Medellín.
  • I've been waiting for the bus for twenty minutes.Llevo veinte minutos esperando el autobús.
  • They have been traveling around Peru since last Friday.Han estado viajando por Perú desde el viernes pasado.
  • Why are your shoes so dirty? Have you been hiking?¿Por qué tienes los zapatos tan sucios? ¿Has estado de caminata?
  • Diego hasn't been sleeping well this week because of his new job.Diego no ha estado durmiendo bien esta semana por su nuevo trabajo.
  • How long have you been learning English with that app?¿Cuánto tiempo llevas aprendiendo inglés con esa aplicación?

Errores típicos

I have been waiting since two hours.
I have been waiting for two hours.
Con un periodo de tiempo se usa for; since va con un punto de inicio (since Monday).
She has been work in Costa Rica.
She has been working in Costa Rica.
Después de been el verbo va en -ing: has been working.
We are waiting for the train for an hour.
We have been waiting for the train for an hour.
Para una acción que empezó antes y sigue ahora se necesita have/has been + -ing, no el presente continuo.

Ponte a prueba

  1. Sofía ___ been studying English since January.

  2. How long ___ you been traveling in Chile?

  3. I've been waiting ___ the bus for ten minutes.

  4. They haven't ___ working this week.

  5. Martín is tired because he has ___ running.
