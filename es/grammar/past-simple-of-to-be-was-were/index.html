Pasado simple de "to be": was / were (nivel A1)

El pasado simple de to be tiene dos formas: was (para I, he, she, it) y were (para you, we, they). Lo usas para hablar de estados y situaciones en el pasado, por ejemplo de tu trabajo anterior o de un viaje. Es muy común en conversaciones sobre experiencias pasadas.

  1. Usa was con I, he, she, it: I was at work.
  2. Usa were con you, we, they: They were in Lima.
  3. Para negar, agrega not: was not / wasn't y were not / weren't. Ejemplo: She wasn't at the meeting.
  4. En preguntas, pon was o were antes del sujeto: Were you in Cusco last week?
  5. Respuestas cortas: Yes, I was. / No, they weren't. No uses el verbo completo después de yes o no.
  6. No uses did con to be: di Was he at the office?, no Did he was...?.

Ejemplos

  • I was a receptionist in Bogotá.Yo era recepcionista en Bogotá.
  • My sister was on a trip to Panama last month.Mi hermana estuvo de viaje en Panamá el mes pasado.
  • We were at the airport at six in the morning.Estábamos en el aeropuerto a las seis de la mañana.
  • Carlos and Lucía weren't at work yesterday.Carlos y Lucía no estuvieron en el trabajo ayer.
  • Was your first job in Medellín?¿Tu primer trabajo fue en Medellín?
  • They were very tired after the flight.Ellos estaban muy cansados después del vuelo.

Errores típicos

I were at work yesterday.
I was at work yesterday.
Con I se usa was, no were.
She wasn't go to the conference.
She wasn't at the conference.
Con to be no se agrega otro verbo; usa wasn't + lugar o adjetivo.
Did you were in Quito last year?
Were you in Quito last year?
Las preguntas con to be no usan did: se invierte were y el sujeto.

Ponte a prueba

  1. My brother ___ in Chile last week.

  2. ___ you at the office yesterday?

  3. Ana and Pablo ___ at the meeting.

  4. It ___ a long trip to Buenos Aires.
