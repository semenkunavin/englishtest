Relative clauses: who / which / that (explicación A2)

Las relative clauses (oraciones de relativo) sirven para dar información extra sobre una persona o una cosa sin repetir el sustantivo. En inglés usamos who para personas, which para cosas y that para las dos. Son muy útiles en el trabajo y en los viajes, por ejemplo para describir a un compañero o un lugar.

  1. Usa who solo para personas: the woman who works with me.
  2. Usa which solo para cosas, animales o lugares: the bus which goes to Cusco.
  3. Usa that para personas y cosas en oraciones informales: the man that called you, the hotel that has a pool.
  4. El pronombre va justo después del sustantivo: the colleague who helps me, no the who colleague.
  5. No repitas el pronombre personal: di the friend who lives in Lima, no the friend who he lives in Lima.
  6. El verbo después de who, which o that concuerda con el sustantivo de antes: people who speak English.

Ejemplos

  • I work with a woman who speaks three languages.Trabajo con una mujer que habla tres idiomas.
  • This is the project that we finished last week.Este es el proyecto que terminamos la semana pasada.
  • The hotel which is near the beach has free breakfast.El hotel que está cerca de la playa tiene desayuno gratis.
  • Do you know the guide who showed us Machu Picchu?¿Conoces al guía que nos mostró Machu Picchu?
  • I need a phone that works in Colombia.Necesito un teléfono que funcione en Colombia.
  • The colleagues that I met in Bogotá were very kind.Los colegas que conocí en Bogotá fueron muy amables.

Errores típicos

The man which works in my office is from Quito.
The man who works in my office is from Quito.
Para personas usamos who o that, no which.
The restaurant who is next to the office is cheap.
The restaurant that is next to the office is cheap.
Para cosas y lugares usamos which o that, no who.
My friend who he lives in Santiago has a new job.
My friend who lives in Santiago has a new job.
No repetimos el pronombre he después de who.

Ponte a prueba

  1. I have a coworker ___ helps me with reports.

  2. This is the ticket ___ I bought online.

  3. We stayed in a hotel ___ has a great view of the city.

  4. The tourists ___ arrived today are from Argentina.

  5. She uses an app ___ translates messages.
