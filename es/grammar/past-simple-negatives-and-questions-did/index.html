Pasado simple: negativas y preguntas con did

El past simple sirve para hablar de acciones terminadas en el pasado, como un viaje o una reunión de trabajo. Para negar y preguntar en pasado simple usamos el auxiliar did. El verbo principal siempre vuelve a su forma base: no lleva -ed ni es irregular.

  1. Para negar usa didn't + verbo en forma base: I didn't work, she didn't travel.
  2. Para preguntar usa Did + sujeto + verbo en forma base: Did you call?, Did they arrive?
  3. Con did y didn't el verbo principal no cambia: se dice Did you go?, nunca Did you went?
  4. Las respuestas cortas son Yes, I did o No, I didn't; no se repite el verbo principal.
  5. Para preguntar detalles usa una palabra interrogativa antes de did: Where did you work?, When did she travel?
  6. Did es igual para todas las personas: Did he call?, Did we pay?, Did they stay?

Ejemplos

  • Did you finish the report yesterday?¿Terminaste el informe ayer?
  • I didn't travel to Lima last month.No viajé a Lima el mes pasado.
  • Where did Carla work in 2022?¿Dónde trabajó Carla en 2022?
  • They didn't call the client on Monday.Ellos no llamaron al cliente el lunes.
  • Did you enjoy your trip to Guatemala?¿Disfrutaste tu viaje a Guatemala?
  • He didn't stay at the hotel near the airport.Él no se quedó en el hotel cerca del aeropuerto.

Errores típicos

Did you went to Bogotá?
Did you go to Bogotá?
Después de did el verbo va en forma base, no en pasado.
I didn't worked yesterday.
I didn't work yesterday.
Después de didn't el verbo no lleva -ed.
Did he called the office?
Did he call the office?
El auxiliar did ya marca el pasado; call queda en forma base.

Ponte a prueba

  1. ___ you visit your family in Costa Rica?

  2. She ___ work last Saturday.

  3. Where ___ they stay in Panamá?

  4. I ___ send the email yesterday.

  5. Did you ___ the meeting at nine?
